Flag of New York

State of New York · US states

What the flag looks like

A dark blue field with the state coat of arms: a shield showing the sun rising over the Hudson River with sailing ships, supported by Liberty and Justice, topped by an eagle on a globe, with the ribbons "E Pluribus Unum" and "Excelsior" below.

What it means

Liberty has a discarded crown at her feet to mark independence from Britain, Justice is blindfolded with scales, and "Excelsior" means "ever upward".

How to remember it

New York's shield is flanked by two women, blindfolded Justice and Liberty, on blue; New Jersey's women stand on buff, and Pennsylvania uses horses.

Fun facts

  • In 2020 the state added "E Pluribus Unum" on a scroll beneath the shield of the coat of arms, and so to the flag.
  • The flag's design dates from 1901, but the arms themselves go back to 1778.
