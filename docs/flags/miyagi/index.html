Flag of Miyagi

Officially Miyagi Prefecture

Locally Miyagi-ken · 宮城県

What the flag looks like

A white emblem on a dark green field: the hiragana み (mi) drawn as three bush-clover leaves.

What it means

Miyagino bush clover (Miyaginohagi) is the prefectural flower and み the first sound of Miyagi; the middle leaf stands for lasting growth, the left for harmony and the right for love of home.

How to remember it

Miyagi's white み (mi) curls into three bush-clover leaves on dark green; Mie's み is a straight arrow on bright green.

Fun facts

  • Sendai, the capital, is the largest city in the Tōhoku region.
  • Matsushima Bay, with over 200 pine-covered islets, is counted among the Three Views of Japan.

Flags often confused with Miyagi

  • Mie: Miyagi and Mie both draw the hiragana み (mi) in white on green. Miyagi's curls into three bush-clover leaves on dark green; Mie's is an arrow pointing up from a ring on bright green.
  • Saga: Miyagi and Saga both have a white emblem on dark green. Miyagi's is a curling み made of leaves; Saga's is a six-petalled flower with a red heart.
