Flag of Ehime

Officially Ehime Prefecture

Locally Ehime-ken · 愛媛県

What the flag looks like

A wide green band between two yellow-orange bands, with a white citrus blossom in the center.

What it means

The mandarin orange blossom is the prefectural flower; white stands for simplicity, green for peace and hope, and yellow for happiness.

How to remember it

Ehime is the only striped prefecture flag: orange, green, orange, with a white citrus blossom, for its mandarin groves.

Fun facts

  • Dōgo Onsen in Matsuyama is one of Japan's oldest hot springs.
  • The Shimanami Kaidō bridges link Ehime to Hiroshima across the islands of the Seto Inland Sea.
