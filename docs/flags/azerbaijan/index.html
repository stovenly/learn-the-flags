Flag of Azerbaijan

Officially Republic of Azerbaijan

Azərbaycan

What the flag looks like

Three equal horizontal stripes of blue, red and green, with a white crescent and white eight-pointed star in the centre of the red stripe.

What it means

Blue represents Turkic heritage, red progress and modernisation, and green Islam; the eight points of the star are often linked to the eight branches of the Turkic peoples.

How to remember it

Azerbaijan is blue-red-green with the crescent and eight-pointed star centred in the red stripe; Uzbekistan has a white middle band and its crescent in the blue corner.

Fun facts

  • The flag was first adopted in 1918 by the Azerbaijan Democratic Republic and restored in 1991.
  • Baku lies about 28 metres below sea level, making it the lowest-lying national capital in the world.

Flags often confused with Azerbaijan

  • Uzbekistan: Azerbaijan and Uzbekistan both have blue top and green bottom stripes with a white crescent. Azerbaijan's middle stripe is red with the crescent centred; Uzbekistan's is white, with the crescent and stars in the upper-left corner.
  • Turkey: Azerbaijan and Turkey both show a white crescent and star on red. Turkey's flag is all red; Azerbaijan's red is only the middle of blue, red and green stripes.
