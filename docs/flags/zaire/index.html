Flag of Zaire

Officially Republic of Zaire

Locally République du Zaïre

What the flag looks like

A green field with a yellow disc in the centre, in which a brown arm and hand hold up a torch with a red flame.

What it means

The torch was the emblem of the Popular Movement of the Revolution, the ruling and only legal party under Mobutu Sese Seko.

How to remember it

Mobutu's Zaire flew a green flag with a yellow disc where a hand raises a flaming torch; DR Congo, as it became, flies sky blue with a red diagonal.

Fun facts

  • Mobutu also renamed the Congo River the Zaire River, and the national currency was called the zaire.
  • When Laurent-Désiré Kabila's forces took Kinshasa in 1997, the country became the Democratic Republic of the Congo again.
