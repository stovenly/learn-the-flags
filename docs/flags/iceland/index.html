Flag of Iceland

Ísland

What the flag looks like

A blue field with a red Nordic cross outlined in white, its vertical arm shifted towards the hoist.

What it means

Red is for volcanic fire, white for ice and snow, and blue for the mountains and surrounding sea.

How to remember it

Fire inside ice on a blue sea: Iceland's red cross edged in white on blue is Norway's flag with the red and blue swapped.

Fun facts

  • The flag was first recognised by Denmark in 1915 and became the flag of the republic in 1944.
  • Iceland's parliament, the Althing, was founded in 930 and is one of the oldest in the world.
