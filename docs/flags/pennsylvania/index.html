Flag of Pennsylvania

Officially Commonwealth of Pennsylvania

What the flag looks like

A dark blue field with the state coat of arms: a shield with a ship, a plough and three sheaves of wheat, topped by a bald eagle and supported by two black horses, above crossed stalks of corn and an olive branch and a red ribbon reading "Virtue, Liberty and Independence".

What it means

The ship stands for commerce, the plough and wheat for farming, and the olive branch and corn for peace and prosperity.

How to remember it

Pennsylvania's shield is held up by two rearing black horses, where New York uses two women.

Fun facts

  • Pennsylvania is one of four US states officially styled a commonwealth.
  • The 1907 flag law requires the blue to match the blue of the US flag.
