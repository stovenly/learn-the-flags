Flag of Switzerland

Officially Swiss Confederation

Schweiz

What the flag looks like

A square red flag with a white cross in the centre whose arms do not touch the edges; each arm is one-sixth longer than it is wide.

What it means

The white cross derives from the old banner of the canton of Schwyz and is commonly read as a Christian symbol; the flag stands for the Confederation's unity.

How to remember it

A square red flag with a floating white plus sign: Switzerland is a plus, and its cross never touches the edges, unlike Denmark's.

Fun facts

  • Switzerland and Vatican City have the only square national flags; Swiss boats use a rectangular version.
  • The Red Cross emblem, created in 1864 in Geneva, reverses the colours of the Swiss flag in honour of Switzerland.

Flags often confused with Switzerland

  • Denmark: Switzerland and Denmark both have a white cross on red. Switzerland's flag is square with a short centred cross; Denmark's cross reaches the edges and sits towards the hoist.
  • Tonga: Switzerland and Tonga both show a cross on red and white. Switzerland has a white cross in the centre of a square flag; Tonga has a red cross in a white box in the upper-left corner.
