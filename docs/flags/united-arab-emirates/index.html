Flag of the United Arab Emirates

al-Imārāt · الإمارات

What the flag looks like

A vertical red band at the hoist, with three equal horizontal stripes of green, white and black filling the rest of the flag.

What it means

The Pan-Arab colours are commonly said to stand for fertility (green), peace and neutrality (white), strength (black) and unity and courage (red).

How to remember it

The UAE is the Pan-Arab tricolour with a vertical red bar at the hoist, not a triangle (Jordan, Palestine, Sudan) or a trapezoid (Kuwait).

Fun facts

  • The flag was designed in 1971 by Abdullah Mohammed Al Maainah, who was 19 at the time and won a public design competition.
  • The UAE marks Flag Day every year on 3 November.
  • The federation has seven emirates; Ras Al Khaimah joined in February 1972, a few weeks after the other six.

Flags often confused with the United Arab Emirates

  • Kuwait: The UAE and Kuwait both have green, white and a fourth colour beside a hoist shape. The UAE has a red rectangle and black bottom stripe; Kuwait has a black trapezoid and red bottom stripe.
  • Jordan: The UAE and Jordan both use red, green, white and black. The UAE has a red vertical band with green on top; Jordan has a red triangle with a white star and black on top.
  • Palestine: The UAE and Palestine both use red, green, white and black. Palestine has a red triangle with black on top; the UAE has a straight red vertical band with green on top.
  • Sudan: The UAE and Sudan both use red, green, white and black. Sudan has a green triangle with red on top; the UAE has a straight red vertical band with green on top.
