Flag of Nicaragua

Republic of Nicaragua

What the flag looks like

Three equal horizontal bands of blue, white and blue, with the coat of arms in the centre: a triangle containing five volcanoes, a cap of liberty and a rainbow, circled by the words "REPUBLICA DE NICARAGUA - AMERICA CENTRAL".

What it means

The blue bands represent the Pacific Ocean and the Caribbean Sea and white the land and peace; the five volcanoes stand for the five members of the former Federal Republic of Central America.

How to remember it

Nicaragua circles its rainbow-and-volcanoes triangle with gold lettering; El Salvador wraps its triangle in a laurel wreath, and Honduras just has stars.

Fun facts

  • The rainbow in the coat of arms makes Nicaragua's one of only two national flags that include purple, alongside Dominica's.
  • Lake Nicaragua is one of the few freshwater lakes in the world where bull sharks are found.
