Flag of Western Sahara

Officially Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic

aṣ-Ṣaḥrāʼ al-Gharbiyya · الصحراء الغربية

What the flag looks like

Three horizontal stripes of black, white and green, with a red triangle at the hoist and a red crescent and star in the centre of the white stripe.

What it means

Black, white, green and red are the Pan-Arab colours, and the crescent and star are symbols of Islam.

How to remember it

Western Sahara is Palestine's flag with a red crescent and star added to the white stripe; Jordan puts a white star in the triangle instead.

Fun facts

  • The flag was adopted when the Polisario Front proclaimed the Sahrawi Arab Democratic Republic on 27 February 1976.
  • Western Sahara is the largest territory by area on the UN's list of non-self-governing territories.

Flags often confused with Western Sahara

  • Palestine: Western Sahara and Palestine both have black, white and green stripes with a red triangle at the hoist. Western Sahara adds a red crescent and star on the white stripe; Palestine's stripes are plain.
  • Jordan: Western Sahara and Jordan both have black, white and green stripes with a red triangle at the hoist. Jordan has a small white star in the triangle; Western Sahara has a red crescent and star on the white stripe.
