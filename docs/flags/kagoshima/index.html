Flag of Kagoshima

Officially Kagoshima Prefecture

Locally Kagoshima-ken · 鹿児島県

What the flag looks like

A black horseshoe around a red disc on a white field: a map of the prefecture around Sakurajima.

What it means

The black ring is the Satsuma and Ōsumi peninsulas, with a dent for Shibushi Bay, and the red disc is the volcano Sakurajima.

How to remember it

Kagoshima's black horseshoe holds a red ball: two peninsulas hugging the Sakurajima volcano.

Fun facts

  • Sakurajima, facing the capital across the bay, is one of Japan's most active volcanoes.
  • The flag is now rarely flown: its map leaves out the prefecture's many islands, and a separate symbol flag is used instead.

Flags often confused with Kagoshima

  • Okinawa: Kagoshima and Okinawa both put a round emblem with red on a white field. Kagoshima's is a black horseshoe around a small red ball; Okinawa's is all red with a white ring inside.
