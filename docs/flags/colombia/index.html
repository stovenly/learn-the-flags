Flag of Colombia

Republic of Colombia

What the flag looks like

Three horizontal bands of yellow, blue and red, with the yellow band taking up the top half and the blue and red each a quarter; there is no emblem.

What it means

Yellow is commonly said to represent the nation's wealth, blue its two seas and red the blood shed for independence.

How to remember it

Colombia is the plain one: a double-height yellow band with no emblem; Ecuador adds a condor coat of arms and Venezuela an arc of stars.

Fun facts

  • The yellow, blue and red colours come from the flag of independence leader Francisco de Miranda and are shared with Ecuador and Venezuela, the other heirs of Gran Colombia.
  • Colombia is named after Christopher Columbus and is the only South American country with coastlines on both the Pacific and the Caribbean.
