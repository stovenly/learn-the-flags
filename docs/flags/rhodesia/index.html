Flag of Rhodesia

Republic of Rhodesia · Historical flags

What the flag looks like

Three equal vertical stripes of green, white and green, with the Rhodesian coat of arms in the centre of the white stripe.

What it means

The coat of arms shows a gold pick on a green shield, for mining, and is topped by the Zimbabwe Bird, a carving found at Great Zimbabwe.

How to remember it

Rhodesia, the white-minority state declared in 1965, flew green-white-green vertical stripes with a coat of arms in the centre; Nigeria's green-white-green is plain.

Fun facts

  • Rhodesia's 1965 Unilateral Declaration of Independence from Britain was not formally recognised by any country.
  • The flag was first raised on 11 November 1968, the third anniversary of the declaration, replacing a blue ensign with the Union Jack.
