Flag of United States

United States of America

What the flag looks like

Thirteen horizontal stripes alternating red and white, starting and ending with red, with a blue rectangle in the upper hoist holding 50 white five-pointed stars in nine offset rows of six and five.

What it means

The 13 stripes represent the original colonies and the 50 stars the states; red is traditionally said to signify valour, white purity and blue vigilance and justice.

How to remember it

The United States: 50 stars and 13 stripes; Liberia has 11 stripes and one big star, and Malaysia adds a crescent.

Fun facts

  • The 50-star flag became official on 4 July 1960 and has been in use longer than any earlier version.
  • Six American flags were planted on the Moon during the Apollo landings between 1969 and 1972.
