Flag of Kyoto

Officially Kyoto Prefecture

Locally Kyōto-fu · 京都府

What the flag looks like

A white six-leafed flower on a violet field, holding a yellow 京 (kyō) drawn as a human figure.

What it means

The six leaves stand for the dignity of the old capital, and the 京 in their heart for the people of Kyoto joining their strength.

How to remember it

Kyoto's white flower on violet has a yellow 京 (kyō), for Kyoto, at its heart.

Fun facts

  • Kyoto was Japan's imperial capital for more than a thousand years, from 794 to 1869.
  • The prefecture also reaches north to the Sea of Japan at Amanohashidate.
