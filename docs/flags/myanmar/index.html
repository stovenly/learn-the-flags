Flag of Myanmar

Officially Republic of the Union of Myanmar

Locally Myanma · မြန်မာ

What the flag looks like

Three equal horizontal stripes of yellow, green and red, with a large white five-pointed star in the centre spanning all three.

What it means

Yellow represents solidarity, green peace and tranquillity, and red courage and decisiveness; the white star stands for the union of the country.

How to remember it

Myanmar is yellow-green-red with a huge white star straddling all three stripes; Lithuania has the same order with no star, and Ghana has a black star.

Fun facts

  • The flag was introduced in 2010 along with the 2008 constitution.
  • The government moved the capital from Yangon to the purpose-built city of Naypyidaw in 2005–2006.

Flags often confused with Myanmar

  • Lithuania: Lithuania and Myanmar both have yellow, green and red horizontal stripes. Myanmar has a large white star across all three stripes; Lithuania's flag is plain.
  • Ghana: Ghana and Myanmar both have red, yellow and green stripes with a star. Ghana has red on top and a small black star; Myanmar has yellow on top and a large white star.
  • Bolivia: Bolivia and Myanmar both use horizontal red, yellow and green stripes. Bolivia has red on top; Myanmar has yellow on top and a large white star across all three stripes.
