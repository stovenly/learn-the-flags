Flag of Myanmar

Republic of the Union of Myanmar

What the flag looks like

Three equal horizontal stripes of yellow, green and red, with a large white five-pointed star in the centre spanning all three.

What it means

Yellow represents solidarity, green peace and tranquillity, and red courage and decisiveness; the white star stands for the union of the country.

How to remember it

Myanmar is yellow-green-red with a huge white star straddling all three stripes; Lithuania has the same order with no star, and Ghana has a black star.

Fun facts

  • The flag was introduced in 2010 along with the 2008 constitution.
  • The government moved the capital from Yangon to the purpose-built city of Naypyidaw in 2005–2006.
