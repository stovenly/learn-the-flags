Flag of Tochigi

Officially Tochigi Prefecture

Locally Tochigi-ken · 栃木県

What the flag looks like

A white emblem on a light green field: the kanji 栃 (tochi) squared into a blocky shape, with three upward arrows at the top left.

What it means

The arrows are an ancient form of 木 ("tree"), the left part of 栃, pointing upward for energy and progress.

How to remember it

Tochigi's blocky white emblem on light green is the kanji 栃 (tochi), with three little arrows pointing up in the corner.

Fun facts

  • Nikkō's Tōshō-gū, the lavish shrine of Tokugawa Ieyasu, is in Tochigi.
  • Tochigi has grown more strawberries than any other prefecture for over 50 years.
