Flag of Saint Lucia

What the flag looks like

A cerulean blue field with a central emblem of a gold triangle set in front of a larger black triangle edged in white.

What it means

Blue represents the sky and the surrounding sea, gold the sunshine, and black and white the harmony of the island's peoples; the triangles evoke the twin Pitons volcanic peaks.

How to remember it

Saint Lucia shows its twin Pitons as a gold triangle in front of a white-edged black one on plain sky blue.

Fun facts

  • The flag was designed by Saint Lucian artist Dunstan St Omer.
  • Saint Lucia has produced two Nobel laureates: economist Sir Arthur Lewis (1979) and poet Derek Walcott (1992).
  • The island is named after Saint Lucy of Syracuse, and its national day, 13 December, is her feast day.
