Flag of Finland

Officially Republic of Finland

Locally Suomi

What the flag looks like

A white field with a blue Nordic cross extending to the edges, its vertical arm shifted towards the hoist.

What it means

Blue is commonly linked to Finland's lakes and sky, and white to snow; the cross is a shared Nordic and Christian symbol.

How to remember it

A blue cross on snow-white: Finland's 'blue cross flag' is the only Nordic cross on a white background; Greece keeps its cross in a corner of stripes.

Fun facts

  • In Finnish the flag is called 'Siniristilippu', literally 'blue cross flag'.
  • Finland has about 188,000 lakes, earning it the nickname 'land of a thousand lakes'.
