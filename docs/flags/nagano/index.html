Flag of Nagano

Officially Nagano Prefecture

Locally Nagano-ken · 長野県

What the flag looks like

A white disc on an orange field, split by orange curves into the katakana ナ (na).

What it means

ナ, the first sound of Nagano, is drawn as a bird in flight and as mountains mirrored in a lake; orange is sunlight and white the snow of the "Roof of Japan".

How to remember it

Nagano's white disc on orange is cut into a ナ (na); Fukushima's white emblem on orange is an open ring.

Fun facts

  • Nagano borders eight prefectures, more than any other.
  • The orange of the 1998 Nagano Winter Olympics emblem comes from the flag.

Flags often confused with Nagano

  • Fukushima: Fukushima and Nagano both have a white emblem on orange. Fukushima's is an open ring, a curled ふ, set toward the hoist; Nagano's is a solid disc cut by orange curves.
