Flag of Portugal

Officially Portuguese Republic

What the flag looks like

A vertical bicolour of green at the hoist (two-fifths) and red (three-fifths), with the national arms on a yellow armillary sphere centred on the dividing line.

What it means

Green is commonly said to stand for hope and red for the blood of those who died for the nation; the armillary sphere honours the Age of Discovery.

How to remember it

Portugal splits green and red unevenly and pins a navigator's armillary sphere on the seam, a reminder of its seafaring explorers.

Fun facts

  • The flag was adopted in 1911, after the monarchy was overthrown in 1910.
  • The shield carries seven castles and five small blue shields called 'quinas'.
