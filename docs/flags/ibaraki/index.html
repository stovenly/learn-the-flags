Flag of Ibaraki

Officially Ibaraki Prefecture

Locally Ibaraki-ken · 茨城県

What the flag looks like

A white disc on an indigo field, holding a blue spiral: a rose bud.

What it means

The rose is the prefectural flower, and the spiral stands for progress, creativity and growth.

How to remember it

Ibaraki's white disc on blue holds a spiral rose bud; the name means "thorn castle", and roses have thorns.

Fun facts

  • Mito, the capital, is famous for nattō, fermented soybeans.
  • The present flag replaced an older rose design in 1991.
