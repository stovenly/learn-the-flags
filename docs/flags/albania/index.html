Flag of Albania

Shqipëria

Republic of Albania

What the flag looks like

A red field with a black double-headed eagle in the centre.

What it means

Red stands for bravery, strength and the blood of patriots; the double-headed eagle comes from the seal of the 15th-century national hero Skanderbeg.

How to remember it

A black double-headed eagle spread across a blood-red field: Albania's eagle is black and borderless, while Montenegro's is gold inside a gold frame.

Fun facts

  • Albanians call their country Shqipëri, a name popularly linked to the word for eagle, giving it the nickname 'Land of the Eagles'.
  • The flag was raised in Vlorë on 28 November 1912 when Ismail Qemali declared Albania's independence from the Ottoman Empire.

Flags often confused with Albania
