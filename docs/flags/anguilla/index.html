Flag of Anguilla

Territories

What the flag looks like

A Blue Ensign with the coat of arms in the fly: a white shield with three orange dolphins leaping in a circle above a turquoise base.

What it means

The three dolphins stand for unity, strength and endurance; the white represents peace and the turquoise the surrounding sea.

How to remember it

Anguilla's badge is three orange dolphins chasing each other in a circle: no lion, no ship, no person.

Fun facts

  • The dolphins come from the Dolphin Flag flown during Anguilla's 1967 revolt against rule from St Kitts, which is still widely seen on the island.
  • Anguilla's .ai internet domain has become a major source of government income thanks to the boom in artificial intelligence.

Flags often confused with Anguilla

  • Turks and Caicos Islands: Anguilla and Turks and Caicos are both Blue Ensigns with sea creatures on the badge. Anguilla has three orange dolphins in a circle on white and turquoise; Turks and Caicos has a conch, a lobster and a cactus on a yellow shield.
  • Montserrat: Anguilla and Montserrat are both Caribbean Blue Ensigns with a badge in the fly. Anguilla's badge is three orange dolphins in a circle; Montserrat's is a woman in green with a harp and a cross.
  • Cook Islands: Anguilla and the Cook Islands are both Blue Ensigns with a circle in the fly. Anguilla's circle is three orange dolphins on a white and turquoise shield; the Cook Islands' is a ring of fifteen white stars straight on the blue.
