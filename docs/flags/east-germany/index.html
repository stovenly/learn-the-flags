Flag of East Germany

Deutsche Demokratische Republik

What the flag looks like

Three equal horizontal stripes of black, red and gold, with the state emblem in the centre: a hammer and a pair of compasses inside a wreath of rye wrapped in a black-red-gold ribbon.

What it means

The hammer stood for the workers, the compasses for the intelligentsia and the rye wreath for the farmers.

How to remember it

East Germany is Germany's black-red-gold with a hammer and compasses in a wreath stamped in the middle; plain stripes mean West Germany or Germany today.

Fun facts

  • Until 1959 East and West Germany flew the identical plain black-red-gold tricolour.
  • At the 1960 and 1964 Olympics the two German states competed as one team under a black-red-gold flag with white Olympic rings.
