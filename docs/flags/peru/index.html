Flag of Peru

Officially Republic of Peru

Perú

What the flag looks like

Three equal vertical bands of red, white and red; the state flag adds the coat of arms in the centre of the white band.

What it means

Red is commonly said to represent the blood shed for independence and white peace; tradition says José de San Martín was inspired by a flock of red and white flamingos.

How to remember it

Peru is red-white-red standing up with three equal bands; Canada's white centre is twice as wide and holds a maple leaf.

Fun facts

  • The coat of arms shows a vicuña, a cinchona tree and a cornucopia spilling coins, representing the animal, plant and mineral riches of the country.
  • Lake Titicaca, on the border of Peru and Bolivia, is often described as the highest navigable lake in the world.

Flags often confused with Peru

  • Canada: Canada and Peru both have vertical red-white-red bands. Peru's three bands are equal with no maple leaf; Canada's white centre is twice as wide and holds a red maple leaf.
  • Lebanon: Lebanon and Peru are both red and white with white in the middle. Lebanon's bands are horizontal with a green cedar tree; Peru's are vertical.
  • Austria: Austria and Peru are both red-white-red. Austria's stripes are horizontal; Peru's are vertical.
