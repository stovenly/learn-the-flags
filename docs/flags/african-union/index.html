Flag of the African Union

What the flag looks like

A green field with a green map of Africa in the centre, set in a burst of white rays and ringed by small gold five-pointed stars.

What it means

Green stands for Africa's hopes, gold for its wealth and bright future, white for purity, and the stars for the member states; the map is drawn without borders as a sign of unity.

How to remember it

The African Union puts the whole continent on its flag: a borderless map of Africa glowing with white sunrays on green, inside a ring of gold stars.

Fun facts

  • The African Union replaced the Organisation of African Unity, which had been founded in Addis Ababa in 1963.
  • It is one of the few flags to show a map, along with those of Cyprus and Kosovo.
