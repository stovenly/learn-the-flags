Flag of Mongolia

Mongol Uls · Монгол Улс

What the flag looks like

Three equal vertical bands of red, blue and red, with the gold Soyombo symbol in the hoist-side red band.

What it means

Blue represents the eternal blue sky and red progress and prosperity; the Soyombo combines fire, sun, moon and other elements standing for the nation and its independence.

How to remember it

Mongolia is red-blue-red vertical with a golden totem of fire, sun and moon (the Soyombo) on the left-hand red band.

Fun facts

  • The Soyombo symbol is a character from a script created by the monk Zanabazar in 1686.
  • Ulaanbaatar is the coldest national capital in the world by average annual temperature.
