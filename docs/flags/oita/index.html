Flag of Oita

Officially Oita Prefecture

Locally Ōita-ken · 大分県

What the flag looks like

Three red kanji 大 (ō) joined in a ring on a white field.

What it means

The three 大 stand for trust, work and friendship; joined hand in hand they form a rising sun.

How to remember it

Oita's red ring on white is three 大 (ō) characters holding hands; Saitama's red ring is sixteen jewels.

Fun facts

  • Ōita has more hot spring sources than any other prefecture, many of them in Beppu and Yufuin.
  • Beppu's "hells" are boiling, colored hot springs meant for looking at, not bathing in.
