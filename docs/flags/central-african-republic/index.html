Flag of the Central African Republic

Bêafrîka

What the flag looks like

Four horizontal stripes of blue, white, green and yellow, crossed in the centre by a vertical red band, with a yellow five-pointed star in the upper hoist corner.

What it means

The flag combines the French blue, white and red with the pan-African green, yellow and red to express unity between Europe and Africa; red is the blood shared by all, and the star is a guide toward independence.

How to remember it

The Central African Republic's four horizontal stripes are cut down the middle by one red vertical band, the French and pan-African colours literally crossing.

Fun facts

  • The flag was designed by independence leader Barthélemy Boganda, who hoped for a wider union of Central African states.
  • The capital, Bangui, lies on the Ubangi River, which forms the border with DR Congo.
