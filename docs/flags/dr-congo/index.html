Flag of the DR Congo

Officially Democratic Republic of the Congo

RD Congo

What the flag looks like

A sky-blue field with a red diagonal band, edged in yellow, running from the lower hoist to the upper fly, and a yellow five-pointed star in the upper hoist corner.

What it means

Blue stands for peace, red for the blood of the country's martyrs, yellow for its wealth, and the star for a radiant future.

How to remember it

DR Congo has a sky-blue field with a yellow star in the corner; its neighbour Congo-Brazzaville has no blue and no star.

Fun facts

  • From 1971 to 1997 the country was called Zaire and flew a green flag showing a hand holding a flaming torch.
  • It is the second-largest country in Africa by area, after Algeria.

Flags often confused with the DR Congo

  • Republic of the Congo: DR Congo and the Republic of the Congo both have a rising diagonal band. DR Congo is sky blue with a red band and a yellow star; the Republic of the Congo is green, yellow and red with no star.
  • Tanzania: DR Congo and Tanzania both have a yellow-edged diagonal band from bottom-left to top-right. DR Congo's band is red on sky blue with a star; Tanzania's is black, between green and blue triangles.
  • Namibia: DR Congo and Namibia both have a red diagonal band from bottom-left to top-right. DR Congo is all sky blue with a yellow star; Namibia has blue and green triangles and a gold sun.
