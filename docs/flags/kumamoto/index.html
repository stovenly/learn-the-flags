Flag of Kumamoto

Officially Kumamoto Prefecture

Locally Kumamoto-ken · 熊本県

What the flag looks like

A white ring with a flat side at the hoist, on a dark red field: the katakana ク (ku) drawn as the island of Kyūshū.

What it means

ク is the first sound of Kumamoto, and the shape is Kyūshū, with the hole marking Kumamoto at its heart.

How to remember it

Kumamoto's white ク (ku) on dark red is also the outline of Kyūshū, with a hole where Kumamoto sits.

Fun facts

  • Mount Aso has one of the largest volcanic calderas in the world.
  • Kumamon, the black bear mascot, is Kumamoto's best-known ambassador.

Flags often confused with Kumamoto

  • Kochi: Kochi and Kumamoto both have a white ring on dark red. Kochi's ring is closed with a red blade pointing up inside; Kumamoto's is a ク with a flat side open toward the hoist.
