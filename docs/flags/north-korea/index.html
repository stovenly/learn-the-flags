Flag of North Korea

Officially Democratic People's Republic of Korea

Locally Chosŏn · 조선

What the flag looks like

A wide red central band bordered above and below by thin white stripes and then blue stripes, with a white disc containing a red five-pointed star in the red band towards the hoist.

What it means

Red represents revolutionary traditions, white purity and unity, and blue sovereignty and peace; the red star stands for communism.

How to remember it

North Korea has a red star inside a white circle on a broad red band between blue stripes; South Korea is a white flag with a red-and-blue yin-yang.

Fun facts

  • The village of Kijong-dong near the border with South Korea has a flagpole about 160 metres tall, one of the tallest in the world.
  • North Korea officially calls itself the Democratic People's Republic of Korea (DPRK).

Flags often confused with North Korea
