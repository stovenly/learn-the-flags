Flag of Wakayama

Officially Wakayama Prefecture

Locally Wakayama-ken · 和歌山県

What the flag looks like

A navy emblem on a white field: the katakana ワ (wa) drawn as an open folding fan.

What it means

ワ is the first sound of Wakayama, and the widening fan stands for endless growth.

How to remember it

Wakayama's navy ワ (wa) on white opens like a fan; Nagasaki's blue N on white is a dove.

Fun facts

  • The Kumano Kodō pilgrimage routes and Mount Kōya are UNESCO World Heritage Sites.
  • Wakayama grows more mandarin oranges than any other prefecture.
