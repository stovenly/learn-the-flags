Flag of Russia

Officially Russian Federation

Rossiya · Россия

What the flag looks like

Three equal horizontal stripes of white, blue and red.

What it means

There is no official meaning; a common interpretation is white for nobility and frankness, blue for faithfulness, and red for courage.

How to remember it

Russia is plain white-blue-red from the top; Slovakia and Slovenia add shields, and Serbia turns it upside down.

Fun facts

  • The white-blue-red tricolour dates back to the late 17th century and is often linked to Peter the Great.
  • Russia celebrates National Flag Day on 22 August.

Flags often confused with Russia

  • Slovakia: Russia and Slovakia are both white, blue and red horizontal stripes. Slovakia has a red shield with a white double cross on the hoist side; Russia's flag is plain.
  • Slovenia: Russia and Slovenia are both white, blue and red horizontal stripes. Slovenia has a small blue shield with a white mountain in the upper hoist; Russia's flag is plain.
  • Serbia: Serbia and Russia both use red, blue and white horizontal stripes. Serbia has red on top and a coat of arms; Russia has white on top and no emblem.
