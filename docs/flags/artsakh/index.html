Flag of Artsakh

Officially Republic of Artsakh

Locally Artsakh · Արցախ

What the flag looks like

Three equal horizontal stripes of red, blue and orange, broken by a white stepped chevron, like a carpet pattern, that is cut into the fly and points toward the hoist.

What it means

The red, blue and orange are the colours of Armenia; the white stepped arrow is said to stand for the region's separation from Armenia and its hope of union, and its shape echoes local carpet designs.

How to remember it

Artsakh took Armenia's red-blue-orange tricolour and cut a white zigzag arrow, like a carpet pattern, into the fly.

Fun facts

  • The Republic of Artsakh was not recognised by any member state of the United Nations, including Armenia.
  • After an Azerbaijani military offensive in September 2023, its leaders signed a decree dissolving the republic, and most of its ethnic Armenian population left for Armenia.
