Flag of Estonia

Eesti

Republic of Estonia

What the flag looks like

Three equal horizontal stripes of blue, black and white.

What it means

Blue is commonly said to represent the sky, sea and lakes, black the soil and the nation's hard past, and white snow, birch bark and hope.

How to remember it

Sky over black earth over snow: Estonia reads like a winter landscape, and no other flag puts black between blue and white.

Fun facts

  • The tricolour began as the flag of the Estonian Students' Society, consecrated in Otepää in 1884, and became the national flag in 1918.
  • It was banned under Soviet rule and raised again on Tallinn's Tall Hermann tower in February 1989.
