Flag of Tonga

Officially Kingdom of Tonga

What the flag looks like

A red field with a white rectangle in the upper hoist containing a red cross.

What it means

The cross represents Christianity and the red the blood of Christ.

How to remember it

A red field with a small red cross in a white corner box: Tonga's flag looks like a Red Cross banner, and its constitution says it may never change.

Fun facts

  • Tonga's constitution states that the flag shall never be altered.
  • An earlier version was white with a red cross, but it was changed because it looked too much like the Red Cross flag.
  • Tonga was never fully colonised: it was a British protected state from 1900 to 1970 but kept its own monarchy throughout.
