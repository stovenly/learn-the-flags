Flag of Wales

Cymru

What the flag looks like

Two equal horizontal bands of white over green, with a large red dragon striding across both.

What it means

The red dragon, Y Ddraig Goch, has been a symbol of Wales for centuries, and white and green were the colours of the Welsh-descended House of Tudor.

How to remember it

Wales has a red dragon striding over white and green; Bhutan's dragon is white and lies across a diagonal split of yellow and orange.

Fun facts

  • Wales is the only one of the UK's countries whose flag is not represented on the Union Jack.
  • The red dragon is said to come from the legend of a red dragon defeating a white one, told in the ninth-century Historia Brittonum.
