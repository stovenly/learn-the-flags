Flag of Romania

România

What the flag looks like

Three equal vertical stripes of blue, yellow and red from the hoist.

What it means

The colours come from the historical principalities of Wallachia and Moldavia, and are popularly said to stand for liberty, justice and fraternity.

How to remember it

Romania is plain blue-yellow-red with a bright cobalt blue; Chad's blue is a darker indigo, and Andorra and Moldova carry a crest.

Fun facts

  • During the 1989 revolution, protesters cut the communist coat of arms out of the centre of the flag.
  • Romania's flag is almost identical to Chad's; Chad raised the issue at the United Nations in 2004, but Romania kept its flag.

Flags often confused with Romania

  • Chad: Romania and Chad are almost identical blue, yellow and red vertical stripes. The only difference is the shade: Chad's blue is darker (indigo), Romania's is brighter.
  • Andorra: Andorra and Romania are both vertical blue, yellow and red. Romania's flag is plain, while Andorra has a coat of arms on a slightly wider yellow centre band.
  • Moldova: Moldova and Romania are both blue, yellow and red vertical stripes. Moldova has an eagle coat of arms in the centre; Romania's flag is plain.
