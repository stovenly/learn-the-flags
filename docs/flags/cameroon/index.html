Flag of Cameroon

Officially Republic of Cameroon

Cameroun

What the flag looks like

Three vertical stripes of green (hoist), red and yellow, with a yellow five-pointed star in the centre of the red stripe.

What it means

Green represents the forests of the south, yellow the savanna and sun of the north, and red unity; the star is the 'star of unity'.

How to remember it

Cameroon puts red in the middle with a yellow star; Senegal puts yellow in the middle with a green star, and Mali has no star at all.

Fun facts

  • From 1961 to 1975 the flag had two stars, for the former French and British Cameroons; they became one when the federation ended.
  • Cameroon is nicknamed 'Africa in miniature' for its range of landscapes, from rainforest to desert to volcanic Mount Cameroon (about 4,040 m).

Flags often confused with Cameroon

  • Senegal: Cameroon and Senegal both have vertical stripes starting with green and a star in the middle. Cameroon's middle stripe is red with a yellow star; Senegal's is yellow with a green star.
  • Mali: Cameroon and Mali both have vertical stripes starting with green. Cameroon's middle stripe is red with a yellow star; Mali's middle stripe is yellow with no star.
  • Guinea: Cameroon and Guinea are both vertical green, red and yellow. Cameroon goes green, red, yellow with a yellow star; Guinea goes red, yellow, green with no star.
