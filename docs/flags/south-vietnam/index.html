Flag of South Vietnam

Việt Nam Cộng hòa

Republic of Vietnam · Historical flags

What the flag looks like

A yellow field with three thin horizontal red stripes across the centre.

What it means

Yellow represented the Vietnamese people and land, and the three red stripes the three regions of Vietnam: north, centre and south.

How to remember it

South Vietnam is yellow with three thin red stripes through the middle; the Vietnam that absorbed it in 1975 flips the scheme to a yellow star on red.

Fun facts

  • The flag is still widely flown by Vietnamese communities abroad, especially in the United States, as the "Heritage and Freedom Flag".
  • After reunification, Saigon was renamed Ho Chi Minh City in 1976.
