Flag of Washington

Officially State of Washington

What the flag looks like

A dark green field with the state seal in the centre: a portrait of George Washington inside a gold ring reading "The Seal of the State of Washington 1889".

What it means

The green stands for the Evergreen State's forests, and the portrait honours the president the state is named after.

How to remember it

Washington is the only green state flag, with George Washington's face looking out from the middle.

Fun facts

  • Washington's is the only US state flag with a green field.
  • It is also the only state flag to show the portrait of a US president.
