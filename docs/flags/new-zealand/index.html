Flag of New Zealand

Aotearoa

What the flag looks like

A dark blue field with the Union Jack in the upper hoist and four red five-pointed stars with white borders in the fly, forming the Southern Cross.

What it means

The Union Jack reflects New Zealand's historical ties to Britain and the Southern Cross its location in the South Pacific.

How to remember it

New Zealand: four red stars outlined in white and nothing under the Union Jack; Australia has white stars, five in its Southern Cross plus a big one below the Jack.

Fun facts

  • In a 2016 referendum about 57% of voters chose to keep the current flag over a silver fern design.
  • The flag's Southern Cross has four stars, leaving out the small fifth star that appears on Australia's.
  • In 1893 New Zealand became the first self-governing country to give women the vote in national elections.
