Flag of Ceuta

Officially Autonomous City of Ceuta

What the flag looks like

A field divided into eight triangles alternating black and white, like a pinwheel, with Ceuta's coat of arms in the centre: the crowned shield of Portugal.

What it means

The black-and-white gyronny is the flag of Lisbon and the shield is Portugal's, a legacy of the Portuguese capture of Ceuta in 1415.

How to remember it

Ceuta flies Lisbon's black-and-white pinwheel with Portugal's shield in the middle: a Spanish city in Portuguese colours.

Fun facts

  • Portugal captured Ceuta in 1415; the city stayed with the Spanish crown when Portugal regained its independence in 1640.
  • It is one of two Spanish cities on the North African mainland, the other being Melilla.
