Flag of Wallis and Futuna

Officially Territory of the Wallis and Futuna Islands

Wallis-et-Futuna

What the flag looks like

A red field with the French tricolour, outlined in white, in the upper hoist corner, and in the fly a white saltire-like emblem of four triangles whose points meet in the middle. It is a local flag; the official flag is the French tricolour.

What it means

The red field and white emblem come from the traditional royal flag of Uvea (Wallis); the tricolour in the corner marks the tie with France.

How to remember it

Wallis and Futuna is a red flag with a little French tricolour tucked in its corner and a white four-triangle cross floating in the fly.

Fun facts

  • The territory keeps three traditional kingdoms, Uvea on Wallis and Alo and Sigave on Futuna, whose kings are recognised under French law.
  • More Wallisians and Futunans live in New Caledonia than in Wallis and Futuna itself.
