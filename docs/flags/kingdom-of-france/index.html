Flag of the Kingdom of France

Royaume de France

What the flag looks like

A white field strewn with gold fleurs-de-lis, with the royal arms in the centre: a blue shield with three gold fleurs-de-lis under the royal crown, held by two angels and encircled by the collars of the Orders of Saint Michael and the Holy Spirit.

What it means

White was the colour of the French monarchy, and three gold fleurs-de-lis on blue had been the arms of the kings of France since the Middle Ages.

How to remember it

Royal France is a white flag sprinkled with gold lilies around a crowned blue shield held up by two angels; the Revolution swapped it for the blue-white-red tricolour.

Fun facts

  • The Order of the Holy Spirit, whose collar hangs around the shield, was founded by Henry III in 1578 and was the highest order of chivalry in the French monarchy.
  • A royal restoration in 1873 failed partly because the Count of Chambord, the Bourbon claimant, refused to give up the white flag for the tricolour.
