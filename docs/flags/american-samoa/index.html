Flag of American Samoa

Officially Territory of American Samoa

Locally Amerika Sāmoa

What the flag looks like

A dark blue field divided by a red-edged white triangle that points to the hoist from a base along the fly, bearing a bald eagle that grips a war club and a fly whisk.

What it means

The eagle represents the United States, while the Samoan war club (uatogi) and fly whisk (fue) in its talons stand for the power of the state and the wisdom of Samoan chiefs.

How to remember it

American Samoa drives a white wedge into the flagpole, and the American eagle riding it clutches two Samoan chiefly symbols.

Fun facts

  • American Samoa is the only inhabited U.S. territory south of the equator.
  • People born in American Samoa are U.S. nationals but not automatically U.S. citizens.
