Flag of Luxembourg

Officially Grand Duchy of Luxembourg

Locally Lëtzebuerg

What the flag looks like

Three equal horizontal stripes of red, white and light blue.

What it means

The colours come from the coat of arms of the House of Luxembourg: a red lion on a field of blue and white stripes.

How to remember it

'Lux' means light, so Luxembourg has the light, sky-blue bottom stripe; the Netherlands uses dark navy.

Fun facts

  • Because the flag is so easily confused with the Dutch one, Luxembourg has repeatedly debated replacing it with the 'Red Lion' flag taken from its coat of arms.
  • Luxembourg is the world's only remaining grand duchy.

Flags often confused with Luxembourg

  • Netherlands: Luxembourg and the Netherlands are both red, white and blue horizontal stripes. Luxembourg's bottom stripe is a lighter sky blue; the Netherlands' is a darker blue.
  • Paraguay: Luxembourg and Paraguay are both red, white and blue horizontal stripes. Paraguay has a round emblem in the centre and a darker blue; Luxembourg's is plain, with light sky blue.
  • Croatia: Croatia and Luxembourg are both red, white and blue. Croatia has a large checkerboard shield in the centre; Luxembourg is plain with a lighter blue.
