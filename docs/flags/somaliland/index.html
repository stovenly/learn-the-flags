Flag of Somaliland

Officially Republic of Somaliland

Arḍ aṣ-Ṣūmāl · أرض الصومال

What the flag looks like

Three equal horizontal stripes of green, white and red, with the Shahada in white Arabic script on the green and a black five-pointed star in the centre of the white.

What it means

The Shahada, the Islamic declaration of faith, affirms the place of Islam; white stands for peace and red for the sacrifices of the struggle for independence.

How to remember it

Somaliland is green-white-red with Arabic script on the green and a black star in the middle; Iran's red emblem sits on the white instead.

Fun facts

  • The territory was briefly independent as the State of Somaliland for five days in 1960 before uniting with Somalia.
  • Somaliland declared independence from Somalia in 1991 and has had its own elected governments since.

Flags often confused with Somaliland

  • Iran: Iran and Somaliland are both green, white and red horizontal stripes with Arabic writing. Iran has a red emblem in the centre and script along the stripe edges; Somaliland has white script on the green and a black star in the centre.
