Flag of Netherlands

Nederland

Kingdom of the Netherlands

What the flag looks like

Three equal horizontal stripes of red, white and blue.

What it means

It derives from the orange-white-blue 'Prince's Flag' of William of Orange; the orange was gradually replaced by red in the 17th century.

How to remember it

The Dutch flag is red-white-blue with a dark navy bottom; Luxembourg's bottom is light sky-blue, and Paraguay adds a crest in the middle.

Fun facts

  • On King's Day and royal occasions the flag is flown with an orange pennant above it, honouring the House of Orange.
  • About a quarter of the Netherlands lies below sea level.
