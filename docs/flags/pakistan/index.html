Flag of Pakistan

Pākistān · پاکستان

Islamic Republic of Pakistan

What the flag looks like

A dark green field with a white vertical band at the hoist and a white crescent and five-pointed star in the centre of the green.

What it means

Green represents the Muslim majority and white the religious minorities; the crescent stands for progress and the star for light and knowledge.

How to remember it

Pakistan is a white crescent and star on dark green with a white strip at the hoist; Algeria is half green, half white with a red crescent.

Fun facts

  • The flag was adopted on 11 August 1947, three days before independence, and was based on the flag of the All-India Muslim League.
  • K2, the second-highest mountain on Earth, lies on the border between Pakistan and China.
