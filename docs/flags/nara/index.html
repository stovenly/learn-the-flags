Flag of Nara

Officially Nara Prefecture

Locally Nara-ken · 奈良県

What the flag looks like

A dark red, nearly round emblem on a white field: the katakana ナ (na) drawn as a circle crossed by a bar.

What it means

ナ is the first sound of Nara; the outer circle is the nature of old Yamato, the inner circle harmony, and the bar steady progress.

How to remember it

Nara's flag is a dark red ball on white with a bar poking out on each side: a rounded ナ (na).

Fun facts

  • Nara was Japan's capital from 710 to 784.
  • Tōdai-ji, home to a giant bronze Buddha, is in Nara, where deer roam the park freely.
