Flag of Tunisia

Officially Republic of Tunisia

Tūnis · تونس

What the flag looks like

A red field with a white disc in the centre containing a red crescent that encircles a red five-pointed star.

What it means

Red recalls the blood of the martyrs and white peace; the crescent and star are symbols of Islam and the country's Ottoman heritage.

How to remember it

Tunisia keeps its red crescent and star inside a white disc in the centre; Turkey's white crescent floats freely toward the hoist.

Fun facts

  • The flag dates from 1831 under Hussein II Bey, making it one of the oldest national flags still in use; its exact proportions were standardised in 1999.
  • The desert planet Tatooine in Star Wars is named after the Tunisian town of Tataouine, and scenes were filmed in southern Tunisia.

Flags often confused with Tunisia

  • Turkey: Tunisia and Turkey both show a crescent and star on red. Tunisia has a red crescent and star inside a white disc in the centre; Turkey's white crescent and star sit directly on the red.
