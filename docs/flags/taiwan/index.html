Flag of Taiwan

Officially Republic of China

Locally Táiwān · 臺灣

What the flag looks like

A red field with a blue canton containing a white sun with twelve triangular rays.

What it means

The colors are linked to Sun Yat-sen's Three Principles of the People: blue for nationalism and liberty, white for democracy and equality, and red for the people's livelihood; the sun's twelve rays represent the twelve months and twelve traditional two-hour periods of the day.

How to remember it

Taiwan is red with a blue top-left corner holding a twelve-rayed white sun ("blue sky, white sun"); Samoa's blue corner holds the Southern Cross stars.

Fun facts

  • The blue canton with the white sun was designed by Lu Haodong in 1895.
  • At the Olympic Games, Taiwan competes as "Chinese Taipei" under a special flag rather than this one.
