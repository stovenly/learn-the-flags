Flag of Gifu

Officially Gifu Prefecture

Locally Gifu-ken · 岐阜県

What the flag looks like

A green ring on a white field, holding a squared-off green kanji 岐 (gi).

What it means

The character 岐 begins the prefecture's name, and the green stands for the beauty of its nature.

How to remember it

Gifu's flag is a green 岐 (gi) in a green ring on white, like a rubber stamp of its own name.

Fun facts

  • The Battle of Sekigahara, which sealed Tokugawa rule in 1600, was fought in Gifu.
  • The thatched farmhouses of Shirakawa-gō are a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
