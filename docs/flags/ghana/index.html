Flag of Ghana

Republic of Ghana

What the flag looks like

Three horizontal stripes of red, gold and green, with a black five-pointed star in the centre of the gold stripe.

What it means

Red represents the blood shed for independence, gold the country's mineral wealth and green its forests; the black star is the lodestar of African freedom.

How to remember it

Ghana's Black Star sits on the gold stripe, the same Black Star that names its football team; Bolivia's red-yellow-green has no star.

Fun facts

  • The flag was designed by Theodosia Okoh; from 1964 to 1966 the middle stripe was white, then gold returned.
  • Ghana's national football team is nicknamed the Black Stars after the flag.
  • Lake Volta in Ghana is the world's largest artificial reservoir by surface area.

Flags often confused with Ghana

BoliviaBolivia and Ghana are both red, yellow and green from top to bottom. Ghana has a black star in the centre; Bolivia has a coat of arms or no emblem.LithuaniaGhana and Lithuania both have red, yellow and green stripes. Ghana has red on top and a black star; Lithuania has yellow on top and no emblem.EthiopiaEthiopia and Ghana both use green, yellow and red stripes with a star. Ethiopia has green on top and a blue disc; Ghana has red on top and a plain black star.Guinea-BissauGhana and Guinea-Bissau both use red, yellow, green and a black star. Ghana has three horizontal stripes with the star in the middle; Guinea-Bissau has a vertical red band at the hoist holding the star.MyanmarGhana and Myanmar both have red, yellow and green stripes with a star. Ghana has red on top and a small black star; Myanmar has yellow on top and a large white star.São Tomé and PríncipeGhana and São Tomé and Príncipe both have black stars on a yellow stripe. Ghana has one star with red on top; São Tomé has two stars, green top and bottom, and a red triangle.
