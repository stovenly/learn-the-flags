Flag of Curaçao

Officially Country of Curaçao

Locally Kòrsou

What the flag looks like

A blue field with a horizontal yellow stripe just below the middle and two white five-pointed stars in the upper hoist, the smaller one nearer the corner.

What it means

Blue stands for the sea and sky and yellow for the sun; the two stars are Curaçao and its uninhabited neighbour Klein Curaçao, and their five points recall the five continents its people came from.

How to remember it

Curaçao is a deep blue flag cut by one yellow line, with two white stars, one big and one small, floating above it like the island and its little sister.

Fun facts

  • Curaçao celebrates Flag Day on 2 July, the date the flag was adopted in 1984.
  • It became a constituent country of the Kingdom of the Netherlands on 10 October 2010, when the Netherlands Antilles was dissolved.

Flags often confused with Curaçao

  • Aruba: Aruba and Curaçao are both blue with yellow horizontal lines low down and stars near the hoist. Aruba is light blue with two yellow lines and one red four-pointed star; Curaçao is dark blue with one yellow stripe and two white five-pointed stars.
