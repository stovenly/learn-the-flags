Flag of Molossia

Officially Republic of Molossia

What the flag looks like

Three equal horizontal stripes of blue, white and green.

What it means

Known as the Grand Triune, the flag is Sierra Leone's tricolour turned upside down.

How to remember it

Molossia is blue-white-green from the top, exactly Sierra Leone's flag flipped over.

Fun facts

  • Kevin Baugh runs the micronation from his property of about 4.6 hectares near Dayton, Nevada, and calls the property taxes he pays to Storey County foreign aid.
  • Molossia issues its own currency, the valora.

Flags often confused with Molossia

  • Sierra Leone: Molossia and Sierra Leone are the same three stripes in reverse order. Sierra Leone has green on top and blue at the bottom; Molossia has blue on top and green at the bottom.
