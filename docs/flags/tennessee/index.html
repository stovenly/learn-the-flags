Flag of Tennessee

Officially State of Tennessee

What the flag looks like

A red field with a blue disc edged in white in the centre, holding three white five-pointed stars; at the fly end is a narrow white stripe and a broader blue vertical stripe.

What it means

The three stars stand for East, Middle and West Tennessee, the state's three Grand Divisions, bound together by the unbroken circle.

How to remember it

Tennessee is three white stars in a blue circle on red, one for each of its three Grand Divisions.

Fun facts

  • The flag was designed by LeRoy Reeves, an officer of the Third Tennessee Infantry.
  • Its designer said the blue bar at the fly stops the flag showing nothing but red when it hangs limp.
