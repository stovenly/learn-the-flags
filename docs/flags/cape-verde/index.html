Flag of Cape Verde

Officially Republic of Cabo Verde

Cabo Verde

What the flag looks like

A blue field with three narrow horizontal stripes, white, red and white, below the centre, and a ring of ten yellow five-pointed stars centred on them toward the hoist.

What it means

The ten stars are the ten main islands; blue is the ocean and sky, and the stripes represent the road toward building the nation, with white for peace and red for effort.

How to remember it

Cape Verde's ring of ten yellow stars, one per island, floats off-centre on a blue sea, like a European Union flag drifting toward the hoist.

Fun facts

  • Before 1992 Cape Verde's flag closely resembled Guinea-Bissau's, as both came from the same independence party.
  • The islands were uninhabited when Portuguese navigators arrived in the mid-15th century.
  • Singer Cesária Évora, the 'barefoot diva', came from Mindelo on São Vicente.
