Flag of Slovakia

Slovak Republic

What the flag looks like

Three equal horizontal stripes of white, blue and red, with the coat of arms on the hoist side: a white double cross standing on three blue hills on a red shield edged in white.

What it means

White, blue and red are pan-Slavic colours; the double cross reflects the Christian heritage brought by Saints Cyril and Methodius, and the three hills are traditionally the Tatra, Fatra and Mátra ranges.

How to remember it

Slovakia's large red shield carries a white double cross on three blue hills; Slovenia's smaller blue shield shows a mountain and stars.

Fun facts

  • The flag was adopted in September 1992, a few months before Czechoslovakia peacefully split on 1 January 1993.
  • Without the shield, Slovakia's flag would be identical to Russia's.
