Flag of Turkey

Republic of Türkiye

What the flag looks like

A red field with a white crescent, opening towards the fly, and a white five-pointed star just beyond it, both slightly towards the hoist.

What it means

A popular legend says the design is the reflection of the crescent moon and a star in a pool of blood after a battle; it derives from late Ottoman flags.

How to remember it

Turkey is a white crescent and star on plain red; Tunisia sets its red crescent and star inside a white disc.

Fun facts

  • The exact proportions of the flag were set by the Turkish Flag Law of 1936.
  • Istanbul spans two continents, Europe and Asia, divided by the Bosphorus strait.
