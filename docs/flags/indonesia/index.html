Flag of Indonesia

Republic of Indonesia

What the flag looks like

Two equal horizontal bands, red on top and white below, in a 2:3 proportion.

What it means

Red stands for courage and white for purity; the colours derive from the banner of the medieval Majapahit Empire.

How to remember it

Indonesia is red over white, just like Monaco, but longer (2:3 versus Monaco's squarer 4:5); Poland flips it to white over red.

Fun facts

  • The flag is called Sang Saka Merah-Putih, "the sacred red and white", and was first officially raised on independence day, 17 August 1945.
  • Indonesia is the largest archipelagic country in the world, with more than 17,000 islands.
