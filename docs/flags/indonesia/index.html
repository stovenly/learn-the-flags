Flag of Indonesia

Officially Republic of Indonesia

What the flag looks like

Two equal horizontal bands, red on top and white below, in a 2:3 proportion.

What it means

Red stands for courage and white for purity; the colours derive from the banner of the medieval Majapahit Empire.

How to remember it

Indonesia is red over white, just like Monaco, but longer (2:3 versus Monaco's squarer 4:5); Poland flips it to white over red.

Fun facts

  • The flag is called Sang Saka Merah-Putih, "the sacred red and white", and was first officially raised on independence day, 17 August 1945.
  • Indonesia is the largest archipelagic country in the world, with more than 17,000 islands.

Flags often confused with Indonesia

  • Monaco: Indonesia and Monaco are both red over white. Indonesia's flag is longer (2:3) while Monaco's is nearly square (4:5); Poland flips the colours, white over red.
  • Poland: Indonesia and Poland both have two bands of red and white. Indonesia has red on top; Poland has white on top.
  • Singapore: Indonesia and Singapore are both red over white. Indonesia is plain; Singapore has a white crescent and five stars in the top-left corner.
