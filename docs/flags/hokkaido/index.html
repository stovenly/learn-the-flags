Flag of Hokkaido

Locally Hokkaidō · 北海道

What the flag looks like

A seven-pointed red star edged in white on a dark navy field.

What it means

The seven-pointed star updates the five-pointed North Star of the Meiji-era Development Commission and stands for the pioneer spirit; navy is the northern sea and sky, red the people's unyielding energy, and white the light and the snow.

How to remember it

Hokkaido flies a red seven-pointed star rimmed in white on navy: a North Star for the far north.

Fun facts

  • Hokkaido covers about a fifth of Japan's land area, making it by far the largest prefecture.
  • Sapporo Beer's red star comes from the same North Star of the Development Commission that inspired the flag.
