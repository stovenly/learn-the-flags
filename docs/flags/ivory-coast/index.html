Flag of Ivory Coast

Officially Republic of Côte d'Ivoire

Côte d'Ivoire

What the flag looks like

Three vertical stripes of orange (hoist), white and green.

What it means

Orange represents the savanna of the north, white peace and unity, and green hope and the forests of the south.

How to remember it

Ivory Coast is Ireland in a mirror: orange savanna at the flagpole, green coastal forest at the fly, while Ireland starts with green.

Fun facts

  • Besides the reversed colour order, Ivory Coast's flag is shorter than Ireland's: proportions 2:3 against Ireland's 1:2.
  • Ivory Coast is the world's largest producer of cocoa beans.
  • Yamoussoukro's Basilica of Our Lady of Peace is among the largest churches in the world.

Flags often confused with Ivory Coast

  • Ireland: Ivory Coast and Ireland are both vertical orange, white and green. Ivory Coast has orange at the hoist (left); Ireland has green at the hoist.
  • Niger: Ivory Coast and Niger both use orange, white and green. Ivory Coast's stripes are vertical and plain; Niger's are horizontal with an orange disc in the centre.
  • India: Ivory Coast and India both use orange, white and green. Ivory Coast's stripes are vertical and plain; India's are horizontal with a blue wheel in the centre.
