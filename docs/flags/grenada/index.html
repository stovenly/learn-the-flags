Flag of Grenada

Grenada

What the flag looks like

A red border around a field divided diagonally into yellow triangles at top and bottom and green triangles at hoist and fly; a red disc with a yellow star sits in the centre, three yellow stars run along the top and bottom borders, and a nutmeg appears on the hoist triangle.

What it means

Red represents courage, yellow wisdom and warmth, and green vegetation; the seven stars stand for the seven parishes, and the nutmeg for the island's famous spice crop.

How to remember it

Grenada, the Isle of Spice, wears a small nutmeg on its green triangle, with seven stars inside a red frame.

Fun facts

  • Grenada is known as the "Isle of Spice" and is one of the world's leading producers of nutmeg.
  • Off Grenada's west coast lies an underwater sculpture park, created by artist Jason deCaires Taylor from 2006.
