Flag of Belize

What the flag looks like

A royal blue field with a narrow red stripe along the top and bottom edges and a white disc in the centre holding the coat of arms: two woodcutters beside a mahogany tree, ringed by a wreath of 50 leaves.

What it means

Blue and red are the colours of the two main political parties, joined in a symbol of unity; the woodcutters and mahogany recall the logging trade, and the 50 leaves mark 1950, when the independence movement began.

How to remember it

Belize puts two woodcutters under a mahogany tree in a white disc on blue, with thin red stripes at the top and bottom.

Fun facts

  • Belize is one of very few national flags to show human figures, the two woodcutters in its coat of arms.
  • The motto on the coat of arms, "Sub Umbra Floreo", means "Under the shade I flourish" in Latin.
  • The Belize Barrier Reef is the largest barrier reef in the Northern Hemisphere and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
