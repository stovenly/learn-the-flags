Flag of Kanagawa

Officially Kanagawa Prefecture

Locally Kanagawa-ken · 神奈川県

What the flag looks like

A red kanji 神 (kana) drawn symmetrically on a white field.

What it means

神 is the first character of Kanagawa.

How to remember it

Kanagawa's red emblem on white is the kanji 神 (kana), the first character of its name, drawn as a mirror-image pattern of lines.

Fun facts

  • Yokohama, the capital, is Japan's second-largest city.
  • Kamakura was the seat of Japan's first shogunate, from 1185 to 1333.
