Flag of Gibraltar

What the flag looks like

Two horizontal bands of white over red, the white twice as tall, with a red three-towered castle on the white and a gold key hanging from its gate onto the red.

What it means

The castle and key come from the arms granted to Gibraltar by Spain's Catholic Monarchs in 1502; the key represents Gibraltar as the key to the Mediterranean.

How to remember it

Gibraltar is a white-over-red flag like Poland's, but with a red castle sitting on a tall white band and dangling a golden key into the red.

Fun facts

  • In a 1967 referendum, 12,138 Gibraltarians voted to remain under British sovereignty and 44 voted to pass to Spain.
  • Gibraltar is home to Europe's only wild monkeys, the Barbary macaques.

Flags often confused with Gibraltar

  • Poland: Gibraltar and Poland are both white over red. Poland's bands are equal and plain; Gibraltar's white band is twice as tall and carries a red castle with a gold key hanging into the red.
