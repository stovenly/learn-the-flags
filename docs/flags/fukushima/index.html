Flag of Fukushima

Officially Fukushima Prefecture

Locally Fukushima-ken · 福島県

What the flag looks like

A white hiragana ふ (fu) curled into a ring, set toward the hoist on an orange field.

What it means

ふ is the first sound of Fukushima, rounded for unity and steady progress; its designer said she also drew it as a peach.

How to remember it

Fukushima's white ふ (fu) curls into a peach-shaped ring on orange; Nagano's white disc on orange is cut by curves.

Fun facts

  • Fukushima is the third-largest prefecture by area, after Hokkaido and Iwate.
  • It is one of Japan's leading peach growers.
