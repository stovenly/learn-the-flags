Flag of Mali

Officially Republic of Mali

What the flag looks like

Three vertical stripes of green (hoist), yellow and red.

What it means

Green stands for hope and fertility, yellow for purity and mineral wealth, and red for the blood shed for independence.

How to remember it

Mali is plain green-yellow-red verticals: Guinea in reverse, and Senegal without its green star.

Fun facts

  • The 1959-1961 flag had a black human figure, the kanaga, in the center; it was removed partly because of Islamic objections to images of people.
  • The Great Mosque of Djenné is the largest mud-brick building in the world.

Flags often confused with Mali

  • Guinea: Guinea and Mali are both red, yellow and green vertical stripes in mirror order. Guinea starts with red at the hoist; Mali starts with green.
  • Senegal: Mali and Senegal are both green, yellow and red vertical stripes. Senegal has a green star in the center; Mali's flag is plain.
  • Cameroon: Cameroon and Mali both have vertical stripes starting with green. Cameroon's middle stripe is red with a yellow star; Mali's middle stripe is yellow with no star.
