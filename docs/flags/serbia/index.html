Flag of Serbia

Republic of Serbia

What the flag looks like

Three equal horizontal stripes of red, blue and white, with the coat of arms, a crowned white double-headed eagle on a red shield, placed towards the hoist.

What it means

Red, blue and white are pan-Slavic colours; the double-headed eagle comes from the medieval Nemanjić dynasty.

How to remember it

Serbia is Russia's flag turned upside down, red-blue-white from the top, with a crowned white eagle near the flagpole.

Fun facts

  • Without the coat of arms, Serbia's civil flag is exactly Russia's flag upside down.
  • The coat of arms on the flag was redrawn in 2010.
