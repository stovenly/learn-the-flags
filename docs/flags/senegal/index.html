Flag of Senegal

Sénégal

What the flag looks like

Three vertical stripes of green (hoist), yellow and red, with a green five-pointed star in the centre of the yellow stripe.

What it means

Green represents hope and Islam, yellow wealth and the arts, and red life and sacrifice; the star stands for unity and hope.

How to remember it

Senegal is Mali plus a green Star in the middle: 'S' for Senegal, 'S' for Star.

Fun facts

  • Senegal shares its colours with Mali because both were briefly united in the Mali Federation (1959-1960).
  • Dakar's African Renaissance Monument, at 49 m, is the tallest statue in Africa.
  • Lake Retba near Dakar is famous for its pink water, coloured by algae.

Flags often confused with Senegal

  • Mali: Mali and Senegal are both green, yellow and red vertical stripes. Senegal has a green star in the centre; Mali's flag is plain.
  • Cameroon: Cameroon and Senegal both have vertical stripes starting with green and a star in the middle. Cameroon's middle stripe is red with a yellow star; Senegal's is yellow with a green star.
  • Guinea: Guinea and Senegal both have red, yellow and green vertical stripes. Guinea starts with red and is plain; Senegal starts with green and has a green star in the centre.
