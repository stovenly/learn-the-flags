Flag of the Aosta Valley

Officially Autonomous Region of Aosta Valley

Locally Valle d'Aosta

What the flag looks like

A vertical bicolour of black at the hoist and red in the fly.

What it means

Black and red are the traditional colours of the Aosta Valley.

How to remember it

The Aosta Valley is the plainest flag in Italy: just a black half and a red half side by side.

Fun facts

  • It is the smallest and least populous region of Italy, and both Italian and French are official there.
  • Its capital, Aosta, was founded by the Romans as Augusta Praetoria in 25 BC.
