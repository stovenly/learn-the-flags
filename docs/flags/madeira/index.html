Flag of Madeira

Officially Autonomous Region of Madeira

Locally Região Autónoma da Madeira

What the flag looks like

A vertical triband of blue, gold and blue, with the red-and-white cross of the Order of Christ in the centre of the gold band.

What it means

Blue is said to stand for the ocean around the islands and gold for their warmth; the cross of the Order of Christ recalls the Portuguese voyages of discovery that reached Madeira in the 15th century.

How to remember it

Madeira is blue-gold-blue like Barbados, but the gold stripe carries a red cross of the Order of Christ instead of a black trident.

Fun facts

  • The name means "wood" in Portuguese, after the dense forests the first Portuguese settlers found there around 1420.
  • The cross of the Order of Christ was painted on the sails of Portuguese caravels during the Age of Discovery.
