Flag of Okayama

Officially Okayama Prefecture

Locally Okayama-ken · 岡山県

What the flag looks like

A yellow ring on a dark purple field, with a wing-like bar across the top: the kanji 岡 (oka) drawn in a circle.

What it means

岡 is the first character of Okayama, rounded into a ring for unity and progress.

How to remember it

Okayama's yellow emblem on dark purple is 岡 (oka) bent into a ring with wings.

Fun facts

  • Kōraku-en in Okayama is one of the Three Great Gardens of Japan.
  • Okayama is the home of the folk hero Momotarō, the Peach Boy.
