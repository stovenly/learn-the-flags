Flag of Brunei

Officially Brunei Darussalam

Locally Negara Brunei Darussalam

What the flag looks like

A yellow field crossed by two parallel diagonal bands, white above black, running from the upper hoist to the lower fly, with the red national emblem in the centre.

What it means

Yellow is the colour of the Sultan, while the white and black bands represent his chief ministers; the emblem's crescent stands for Islam, the parasol for the monarchy and the upraised hands for the government's benevolence.

How to remember it

Brunei is the yellow flag slashed by a white-and-black diagonal, with a red crest of a crescent, parasol and two raised hands in the middle.

Fun facts

  • The Arabic motto on the crescent means "Always in service with God's guidance".
  • Sultan Hassanal Bolkiah has reigned since 1967, making him one of the longest-reigning monarchs in the world.
