Flag of the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation

What the flag looks like

A white field with the OIC emblem in the centre: a green crescent curving round a globe drawn in green lines, with the black Kaaba at its heart.

What it means

The Kaaba, the globe and the crescent are the three elements the organisation chose to reflect the vision and mission of its new charter.

How to remember it

The OIC is a green crescent cradling a line-drawn globe with the small black cube of the Kaaba in the middle, all on plain white.

Fun facts

  • The flag was introduced in 2011, when the Organisation of the Islamic Conference renamed itself the Organisation of Islamic Cooperation.
  • Founded in Rabat in 1969, the OIC has 57 member states across four continents.
