Flag of Aichi

Officially Aichi Prefecture

Locally Aichi-ken · 愛知県

What the flag looks like

A white emblem on a muted red field: the hiragana あいち (Aichi) combined into a rising sun over waves.

What it means

The sun over waves shows a prefecture facing the Pacific and the world beyond it.

How to remember it

Aichi's white emblem on muted red is あいち (Aichi) drawn as a sun rising over waves; Hiroshima's white emblem on the same red is a crescent.

Fun facts

  • Toyota is headquartered in Aichi, in a city renamed Toyota after the company.
  • Aichi hosted the 2005 World Expo.
