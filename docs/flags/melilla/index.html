Flag of Melilla

Ciudad Autónoma de Melilla

Autonomous City of Melilla · Territories

What the flag looks like

A light blue field with Melilla's coat of arms in the centre: a crowned shield flanked by the two Pillars of Hercules, under a scroll bearing a Latin motto.

What it means

The coat of arms comes from the Dukes of Medina Sidonia, whose forces took Melilla for Spain in 1497; the Pillars of Hercules are the old symbol of the Strait of Gibraltar.

How to remember it

Melilla is a busy crowned shield between two white columns on a plain sky-blue field.

Fun facts

  • The motto Praeferre patriam liberis parentem decet, "a parent should put the homeland before his children", comes from the house of Medina Sidonia.
  • The coat of arms was granted by King Alfonso XIII in 1913; the flag itself became official with Melilla's autonomy statute in 1995.
