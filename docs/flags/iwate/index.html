Flag of Iwate

Officially Iwate Prefecture

Locally Iwate-ken · 岩手県

What the flag looks like

A white emblem on a dark blue-green field: the kanji 岩 (iwa, "rock") drawn symmetrically top to bottom and side to side.

What it means

The character 岩 begins the prefecture's name, drawn to show a prefecture leaping forward.

How to remember it

Iwate's white emblem is the kanji 岩 (iwa, "rock"), squared off so it reads the same upside down and in a mirror.

Fun facts

  • Iwate is the second-largest prefecture after Hokkaido.
  • Hiraizumi, once the seat of the Northern Fujiwara clan, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
