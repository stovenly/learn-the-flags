Flag of Liechtenstein

Officially Principality of Liechtenstein

What the flag looks like

Two equal horizontal bands, blue over red, with a gold crown in the upper hoist corner.

What it means

Blue is said to represent the sky and red the glow of evening fires in homes; the crown stands for the unity of the people and the princely house.

How to remember it

Blue over red with a gold crown: Liechtenstein added the crown after discovering its plain flag matched Haiti's, so crown means Liechtenstein, central crest means Haiti.

Fun facts

  • At the 1936 Berlin Olympics Liechtenstein discovered its flag was identical to Haiti's civil flag, and added the crown in 1937.
  • Liechtenstein is one of only two doubly landlocked countries, along with Uzbekistan.

Flags often confused with Liechtenstein
