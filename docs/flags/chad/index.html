Flag of Chad

Officially Republic of Chad

Tchad

What the flag looks like

Three vertical stripes of blue (hoist), yellow and red.

What it means

Blue represents the sky, hope and water, yellow the sun and desert, and red progress, unity and sacrifice.

How to remember it

Chad and Romania are near-twins in blue-yellow-red: Chad's blue is a slightly deeper indigo, and Andorra and Moldova add a coat of arms.

Fun facts

  • Chad's flag is almost identical to Romania's; the only difference is a slightly darker shade of blue.
  • Lake Chad has shrunk by around 90% since the 1960s.

Flags often confused with Chad

  • Romania: Romania and Chad are almost identical blue, yellow and red vertical stripes. The only difference is the shade: Chad's blue is darker (indigo), Romania's is brighter.
  • Andorra: Andorra and Chad are both vertical blue, yellow and red. Chad's flag is plain with a dark blue stripe, while Andorra has a coat of arms on a slightly wider yellow centre band.
  • Moldova: Moldova and Chad are both blue, yellow and red vertical stripes. Moldova has an eagle coat of arms in the centre; Chad's flag is plain, with a darker blue.
