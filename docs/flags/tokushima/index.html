Flag of Tokushima

Officially Tokushima Prefecture

Locally Tokushima-ken · 徳島県

What the flag looks like

A yellow emblem on a blue field: the hiragana とく (toku) drawn as a bird in flight.

What it means

とく is the first part of Tokushima, shaped as a bird for unity and soaring growth.

How to remember it

Tokushima's yellow bird on blue spells とく (toku); Tottori's bird on blue is white.

Fun facts

  • The Awa Odori dance festival draws over a million visitors every August.
  • The Naruto whirlpools churn in the strait between Tokushima and Awaji Island.
