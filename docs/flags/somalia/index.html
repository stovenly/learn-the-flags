Flag of Somalia

Officially Federal Republic of Somalia

Locally Soomaaliya

What the flag looks like

A light blue field with a large white five-pointed star in the center.

What it means

The blue was inspired by the United Nations flag and represents the sky and ocean; the five points of the star represent the five regions inhabited by Somali people.

How to remember it

Somalia is a single white star on UN sky blue, one point for each region where Somalis live.

Fun facts

  • The flag was designed in 1954 by Mohammed Awale Liban, while Somalia was a UN trust territory.
  • Somalia has the longest coastline of any country on mainland Africa.
