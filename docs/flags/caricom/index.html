Flag of CARICOM

Officially Caribbean Community

What the flag looks like

Two horizontal bands, light blue over a narrower dark blue, with a yellow disc ringed in green in the centre bearing two black interlocking letters C.

What it means

Light blue stands for the sky and dark blue for the Caribbean Sea; the yellow disc is the sun, the green ring the vegetation, and the linked Cs the Caribbean Community.

How to remember it

CARICOM is sky over sea in two blues, with a yellow sun showing two linked black Cs, for Caribbean Community.

Fun facts

  • CARICOM was founded by the Treaty of Chaguaramas in 1973, signed by Barbados, Guyana, Jamaica and Trinidad and Tobago.
  • Montserrat, a British Overseas Territory, is the only full member that is not a sovereign state.
