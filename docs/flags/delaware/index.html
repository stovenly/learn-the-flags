Flag of Delaware

Officially State of Delaware

What the flag looks like

A colonial-blue field with a buff (tan) diamond holding the state coat of arms, and the date DECEMBER 7, 1787 written beneath the diamond.

What it means

Blue and buff were the colors of George Washington's Continental Army uniform; the date is the day Delaware ratified the US Constitution.

How to remember it

Delaware sets its arms in a tan diamond on blue and signs it with a date, December 7, 1787, the day it became the first state.

Fun facts

  • Delaware was the first state to ratify the US Constitution, which is why it calls itself "The First State".
  • The coat of arms dates from 1777 and shows a farmer and a militiaman beside a shield with a wheat sheaf, an ear of corn and an ox.
