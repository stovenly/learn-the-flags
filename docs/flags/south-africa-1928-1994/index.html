Flag of South Africa (1928–1994)

Unie van Suid-Afrika

Union of South Africa · Historical flags

What the flag looks like

Three equal horizontal stripes of orange, white and blue, with three small flags in the centre of the white stripe: the Union Jack, the flag of the Orange Free State and the flag of the South African Republic (Transvaal).

What it means

The orange, white and blue came from the Dutch Prince's Flag of the early settlers, and the three small flags stood for Britain and the two former Boer republics.

How to remember it

The flag of the Union and of apartheid-era South Africa is orange-white-blue with three tiny flags in the middle; the Netherlands has red on top and no flags.

Fun facts

  • Until 1957 the Union Jack was also an official national flag, flown alongside this one.
  • It was replaced on 27 April 1994, the day of South Africa's first elections open to all races.
