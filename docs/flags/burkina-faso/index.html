Flag of Burkina Faso

What the flag looks like

Two horizontal bands, red over green, with a yellow five-pointed star in the centre.

What it means

Red recalls the revolution and the struggle for liberation, green the country's agricultural wealth and hope, and the yellow star the guiding light of the revolution.

How to remember it

Burkina Faso is a red sky over green fields with one yellow star in the middle, as if Vietnam's flag were standing on a lawn.

Fun facts

  • The flag was introduced in 1984, when Thomas Sankara renamed Upper Volta to Burkina Faso, roughly 'land of upright people'.
  • It replaced a black-white-red tricolour whose stripes stood for the Black, White and Red Volta rivers.
