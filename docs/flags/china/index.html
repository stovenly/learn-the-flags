Flag of China

Zhōngguó · 中国

What the flag looks like

A red field with one large yellow five-pointed star in the canton and four smaller yellow stars arranged in an arc to its right.

What it means

Red represents the communist revolution; the large star stands for the Communist Party and the four smaller stars for the social classes of the people united around it.

How to remember it

China has one big yellow star plus four small ones in the top-left corner; Vietnam has a single big star in the centre.

Fun facts

  • Known as the Five-star Red Flag, it was designed by Zeng Liansong, an economist who entered a national design competition in 1949.
  • Each of the four small stars has one point aimed at the centre of the large star.

Flags often confused with China

  • Vietnam: China and Vietnam are both red with yellow stars. Vietnam has one large star in the centre; China has one large star and four small ones in the upper-left corner.
