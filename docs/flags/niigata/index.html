Flag of Niigata

Officially Niigata Prefecture

Locally Niigata-ken · 新潟県

What the flag looks like

A gold emblem on a red field: the kanji 新 (nii) at the top of a ring formed by the katakana ガタ (gata).

What it means

The emblem spells Niigata, and the ring, like a necklace, stands for harmony, hope and smooth growth.

How to remember it

Niigata's gold ring on red spells its own name: 新 (nii) sits on top of a loop of ガタ (gata).

Fun facts

  • Niigata is famous for Koshihikari rice and for its sake.
  • Sado Island, off the coast, had one of Japan's richest gold mines.
