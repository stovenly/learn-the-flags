Flag of Florida

Officially State of Florida

What the flag looks like

A white field with a red saltire (diagonal cross), with the round state seal at the centre where the arms cross.

What it means

The seal shows a Seminole woman scattering flowers, a sabal palm, a steamboat and a rising sun; the red cross was added in 1900.

How to remember it

Florida is Alabama's red X with a round seal planted in the middle where the bars cross.

Fun facts

  • Governor Francis Fleming is said to have pushed for the red cross in the 1890s so the plain white flag would not look like a flag of surrender.
  • The seal was revised in 1985, swapping a coconut palm for the state's sabal palm and correcting the Seminole woman's headdress.
