Flag of the Red Crescent

What the flag looks like

A red crescent in the centre of a white field, its horns opening towards the fly.

What it means

Like the Red Cross, the emblem marks medical workers and the wounded as protected in war; it is used by the national societies of most Muslim-majority countries.

How to remember it

The Red Crescent is a lone red crescent on plain white; Turkey turns it around, with a white crescent and star on red.

Fun facts

  • The crescent was first used by the Ottoman Empire during the Russo-Turkish War of 1876–1878 and was formally recognised in the 1929 Geneva Convention.
  • A third, religiously neutral emblem, the Red Crystal, was added in 2005.

Flags often confused with the Red Crescent

  • Turkey: The Red Crescent and Turkey both show a crescent opening towards the fly. The Red Crescent is a red crescent alone on white; Turkey is a white crescent and star on red.
  • Red Crystal: The Red Crystal and the Red Crescent are both a single red emblem on white. The Red Crescent is a curved crescent; the Red Crystal is a hollow square stood on its corner like a diamond.
