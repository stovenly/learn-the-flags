Flag of Mie

Officially Mie Prefecture

Locally Mie-ken · 三重県

What the flag looks like

A white emblem on a green field: the hiragana み (mi) drawn as an arrow pointing up, with a ring at its foot.

What it means

み is the first sound of Mie; the arrow stands for progress and the ring for the pearls farmed in Ago Bay.

How to remember it

Mie's white み (mi) shoots up like an arrow from an Ago Bay pearl on bright green; Miyagi's み curls into leaves on dark green.

Fun facts

  • The Ise Grand Shrine, Shinto's holiest site, is in Mie.
  • Iga, in Mie, is famous as a home of the ninja.

Flags often confused with Mie
