Flag of Germany

Officially Federal Republic of Germany

Locally Deutschland

What the flag looks like

Three equal horizontal stripes of black, red and gold.

What it means

The colours trace back to the black uniforms with red trim and gold buttons of the Lützow Free Corps in the Napoleonic Wars, and became the colours of German democracy and unity in 1848.

How to remember it

Germany stacks black, red and gold horizontally with black on top; Belgium stands black, yellow and red upright.

Fun facts

  • Supporters of a united, democratic Germany waved black-red-gold at the Hambach Festival in 1832.
  • East Germany used the same tricolour with a hammer-and-compass emblem added from 1959 until reunification in 1990.

Flags often confused with Germany

  • Belgium: Belgium and Germany both use black, yellow/gold and red. Belgium's stripes are vertical (black, yellow, red); Germany's are horizontal (black, red, gold).
