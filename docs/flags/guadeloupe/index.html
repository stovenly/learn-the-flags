Flag of Guadeloupe

Gwadloup

What the flag looks like

An unofficial but widely used local flag: a black field with a large yellow sun in front of green sugarcane, under a blue band across the top bearing three yellow fleurs-de-lis. The official flag is the French tricolour.

What it means

It is based on the coat of arms of the town of Basse-Terre: the fleurs-de-lis are usually taken to stand for France, and the sun and sugarcane for the island's climate and its sugar plantations.

How to remember it

Guadeloupe is a blazing yellow sun rising out of sugarcane on a black night, with a blue ceiling of three golden fleurs-de-lis.

Fun facts

  • Guadeloupe has no official flag of its own; the Regional Council flies its logo on a white field instead.
  • Seen from above, Guadeloupe's two main islands, Basse-Terre and Grande-Terre, form the shape of a butterfly.
