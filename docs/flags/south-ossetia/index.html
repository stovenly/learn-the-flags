Flag of South Ossetia

Officially Republic of South Ossetia – the State of Alania

Khussar Iryston · Хуссар Ирыстон

What the flag looks like

Three equal horizontal stripes of white, red and yellow.

What it means

The colours are traditionally said to represent moral purity (white), courage (red) and wealth and prosperity (yellow).

How to remember it

South Ossetia is a plain white-red-yellow tricolour from top to bottom, with no emblem at all.

Fun facts

  • The flag has the same design as that of North Ossetia–Alania, a republic of Russia on the other side of the Caucasus.
  • Russia recognised South Ossetia's independence in August 2008, after the Russo-Georgian War.
