Flag of Åland

Officially Åland Islands

What the flag looks like

A blue field with a yellow Nordic cross, inside which runs a narrower red Nordic cross.

What it means

Blue and yellow come from the flag of Sweden, reflecting the islands' Swedish language and culture, while red and yellow are the colors of Finland's coat of arms.

How to remember it

Åland is Sweden's flag with a red cross threaded through the middle of the yellow one: Swedish colors, Finnish red.

Fun facts

  • Åland is demilitarised and, under a 1921 League of Nations decision, belongs to Finland while keeping wide self-government and Swedish as its only official language.
  • It issues its own postage stamps and has its own internet domain, .ax.
