Flag of Macau

Officially Macao Special Administrative Region of the People's Republic of China

Ou Mun · 澳門

What the flag looks like

A green field with a white lotus flower above a stylised bridge and white waves, beneath an arc of five yellow five-pointed stars.

What it means

The lotus is Macau's emblem, its three petals for the peninsula and the islands of Taipa and Coloane; the bridge and water show its setting, and the five stars echo China's flag.

How to remember it

Macau is a white lotus floating on a green field under five gold stars; Hong Kong's flower is a bauhinia on red.

Fun facts

  • The flag was first raised officially on 20 December 1999, when Portugal handed Macau to China after more than four centuries.
  • Macau is among the most densely populated places in the world.

Flags often confused with Macau

  • Hong Kong: Hong Kong and Macau both have a white flower in the centre of a single-coloured field. Hong Kong has a five-petalled bauhinia on red; Macau has a lotus on green, under five yellow stars.
