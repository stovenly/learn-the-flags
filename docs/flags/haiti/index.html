Flag of Haiti

Officially Republic of Haiti

Ayiti

What the flag looks like

Two horizontal bands of blue over red, with a white rectangle in the centre holding the coat of arms: a palm tree topped by a cap of liberty, flanked by flags and cannons above the motto "L'Union Fait La Force".

What it means

The blue and red come from the French tricolour with the white removed, and represent the union of Black and mixed-race Haitians; the motto means "Unity makes strength".

How to remember it

Haiti is blue over red with a white panel holding a palm tree; Liechtenstein shares the blue-over-red but has a gold crown in the corner.

Fun facts

  • According to tradition, the first flag was made on 18 May 1803 when Jean-Jacques Dessalines tore the white out of a French tricolour and Catherine Flon sewed the rest together; 18 May is Flag Day.
  • Haiti became independent in 1804, the first nation founded after a successful revolt by enslaved people.
