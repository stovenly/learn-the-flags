Flag of ASEAN

Officially Association of Southeast Asian Nations

What the flag looks like

A blue field with a red disc edged in white in the centre, holding a bundle of yellow rice stalks tied together.

What it means

The bound rice stalks stand for the countries of Southeast Asia joined in friendship; blue is peace and stability, red courage, white purity and yellow prosperity.

How to remember it

ASEAN is a red disc on blue holding a sheaf of yellow rice stalks tied at the waist, a harvest bundle of Southeast Asian nations.

Fun facts

  • ASEAN was founded by the Bangkok Declaration of 8 August 1967, signed by Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore and Thailand.
  • Timor-Leste became the 11th member on 26 October 2025, the first new member since Cambodia in 1999.
