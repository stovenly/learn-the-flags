Flag of Saga

Officially Saga Prefecture

Locally Saga-ken · 佐賀県

What the flag looks like

A white six-petalled camphor flower with red stamens at its heart on a dark green field.

What it means

The camphor flower is the prefectural flower; the white petals stand for fairness and cleanliness, the red stamens for sincerity and passion.

How to remember it

Saga's white camphor flower on dark green has a red heart; Miyagi's white emblem on dark green is all white.

Fun facts

  • Arita in Saga has made porcelain since the early 1600s.
  • The Yoshinogari site is one of Japan's largest Yayoi-era settlements.
