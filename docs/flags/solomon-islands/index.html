Flag of the Solomon Islands

What the flag looks like

Divided diagonally from lower hoist to upper fly by a thin yellow stripe, with a blue upper hoist triangle containing five white five-pointed stars arranged in an X, and a green lower fly triangle.

What it means

Blue represents the water, green the land and yellow the sunshine; the five stars originally stood for the country's five main island districts.

How to remember it

Five white stars in an X on a blue triangle, then a thin yellow diagonal and green below; Tanzania's diagonal is a wide black band edged in yellow.

Fun facts

  • Guadalcanal, home of the capital Honiara, was the scene of a major Second World War campaign in 1942–43.
  • The country is made up of nearly a thousand islands spread over about 1,500 km of ocean.

Flags often confused with the Solomon Islands

  • Tanzania: Solomon Islands and Tanzania both have a diagonal from lower hoist to upper fly with blue and green. Solomon Islands has blue above, a thin yellow stripe and white stars; Tanzania has green above and a wide black band.
  • Namibia: Namibia and Solomon Islands both split diagonally into blue above and green below. Namibia has a wide red band and a gold sun; Solomon Islands has a thin yellow stripe and five white stars.
