Flag of Niger

Officially Republic of the Niger

What the flag looks like

Three horizontal stripes of orange, white and green, with an orange disc in the centre of the white stripe; the flag is nearly square.

What it means

Orange represents the Sahara in the north, white purity and hope, and green the fertile south and the Niger River; the disc represents the sun.

How to remember it

Niger looks like India with a plain orange sun instead of a blue wheel, on a nearly square flag.

Fun facts

  • Niger's flag has unusual proportions of 6:7, making it almost square.
  • Niger has the highest fertility rate in the world.

Flags often confused with Niger

  • India: India and Niger are both orange, white and green with a disc in the centre. India's disc is a blue wheel; Niger's is a plain orange disc, and its flag is nearly square.
  • Ivory Coast: Ivory Coast and Niger both use orange, white and green. Ivory Coast's stripes are vertical and plain; Niger's are horizontal with an orange disc in the centre.
