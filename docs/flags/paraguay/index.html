Flag of Paraguay

Republic of Paraguay

What the flag looks like

Three equal horizontal bands of red, white and blue, with the national coat of arms in the centre of the white band on the front and the treasury seal, a lion with a cap of liberty, on the back.

What it means

The colours are commonly said to stand for patriotism and courage (red), peace (white) and liberty (blue); the treasury seal's motto "Paz y Justicia" means "Peace and Justice".

How to remember it

Paraguay looks like the Netherlands until you spot the emblem, and flip it over: it is the only national flag with a different design on each side.

Fun facts

  • Paraguay's flag is the only national flag whose two sides differ: the front shows the national arms and the back the treasury seal.
  • Guaraní, an Indigenous language, is spoken by most Paraguayans and is an official language alongside Spanish.
