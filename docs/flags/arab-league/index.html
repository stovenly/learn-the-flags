Flag of the Arab League

Officially League of Arab States

Jāmiʿat ad-Duwal al-ʿArabiyya · جامعة الدول العربية

What the flag looks like

A green field with a white emblem in the centre: an upturned crescent beneath the Arabic name of the League, inside a gold chain and a white laurel wreath.

What it means

The chain stands for the unity of the member states and the wreath for peace; green is a colour long associated with Islam and the Arab world.

How to remember it

The Arab League is green with a white crescent, chain and laurel wreath around Arabic script; Saudi Arabia's green has its script above a sword and no wreath.

Fun facts

  • The League was founded in Cairo on 22 March 1945 by seven states: Egypt, Iraq, Transjordan, Lebanon, Saudi Arabia, Syria and Yemen.
  • Syria's membership was suspended in 2011 and restored in 2023.

Flags often confused with the Arab League

  • Saudi Arabia: The Arab League and Saudi Arabia are both green flags with white Arabic script. The Arab League's script sits above a crescent inside a chain and laurel wreath in the centre; Saudi Arabia has a line of script over a horizontal sword and no wreath.
  • Gulf Cooperation Council: The GCC and the Arab League are both green with a round emblem in the centre. The GCC's emblem is a black ring of script around a map of the Arabian Peninsula in a hexagon; the Arab League's is a white crescent inside a gold chain and laurel wreath.
