Flag of French Polynesia

Locally Polynésie française

What the flag looks like

Three horizontal bands of red, white and red, the white band twice as tall, with a central disc showing a Polynesian outrigger canoe in front of a golden sun, above blue and white waves.

What it means

Red-white-red goes back to the flags of the old Kingdom of Tahiti; the canoe carries five figures for the five archipelagos, sailing between the sun and the ocean.

How to remember it

French Polynesia is Lebanon's red-white-red layout, but instead of a green cedar the white band holds a canoe sailing in front of a golden sun.

Fun facts

  • The five figures in the canoe stand for the Society, Tuamotu, Gambier, Marquesas and Austral archipelagos.
  • Its 118 islands and atolls are scattered across an area of ocean about the size of Western Europe.

Flags often confused with French Polynesia

  • Lebanon: Lebanon and French Polynesia are both red-white-red with a white band twice as tall as the red ones. Lebanon has a green cedar tree in the center; French Polynesia has a canoe in front of a golden sun above blue waves.
  • Austria: Austria and French Polynesia are both red-white-red horizontal stripes. Austria's stripes are equal and plain; French Polynesia's white band is twice as tall and carries a canoe and sun emblem.
