Flag of South Africa

Republic of South Africa

What the flag looks like

A green horizontal Y shape edged in white and gold, whose arms open toward the hoist around a black triangle edged in gold, with a red band above and a blue band below.

What it means

No official meaning is given, but the Y shape is commonly read as diverse elements converging and moving forward together.

How to remember it

South Africa's sideways green Y merges two paths into one road, with a black triangle at the hoist and red above, blue below.

Fun facts

  • The flag was designed by Frederick Brownell just weeks before the 1994 election and was meant to be temporary.
  • South Africa has three capital cities, one for each branch of government.
