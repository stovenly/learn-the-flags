Flag of South Carolina

Officially State of South Carolina

What the flag looks like

An indigo blue field with a white palmetto tree in the centre and a white crescent in the upper hoist corner.

What it means

The crescent comes from the 1775 flag of Colonel William Moultrie's troops, and the palmetto recalls the palmetto-log fort that withstood a British fleet in 1776.

How to remember it

South Carolina is just a white palmetto tree and a crescent moon on indigo, no seal, no words.

Fun facts

  • The soft palmetto logs of Fort Moultrie absorbed British cannonballs during the battle of Sullivan's Island in 1776.
  • The palmetto tree was added in January 1861, weeks after South Carolina seceded from the Union.
