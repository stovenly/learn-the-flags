Flag of Brazil

Officially Federative Republic of Brazil

Brasil

What the flag looks like

A green field with a large yellow rhombus in the centre, holding a blue globe of the night sky with 27 white stars, crossed by a curved white band with the motto "ORDEM E PROGRESSO" in green.

What it means

Green and yellow come from the royal houses of Braganza and Habsburg of the first emperor and empress, and are popularly read as forests and gold; the 27 stars represent the states and the Federal District.

How to remember it

Brazil is the only flag with a starry blue globe inside a yellow diamond on green, wrapped in the banner "Ordem e Progresso".

Fun facts

  • The stars show the sky over Rio de Janeiro on the morning of 15 November 1889, when the republic was proclaimed.
  • The basic design dates from 1889; the star count has been updated as states were created, reaching 27 in 1992.
  • The motto "Ordem e Progresso" (Order and Progress) was inspired by the positivist philosopher Auguste Comte.
