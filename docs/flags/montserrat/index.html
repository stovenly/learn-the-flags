Flag of Montserrat

Territories

What the flag looks like

A Blue Ensign with the coat of arms in the fly: a woman in a green dress holding a golden harp and embracing a dark cross.

What it means

The woman is Erin, the personification of Ireland, honouring the Irish settlers who came to the island in the 17th century.

How to remember it

Montserrat's badge is a woman in green hugging a cross and holding a harp: Ireland's Erin, on the "Emerald Isle of the Caribbean".

Fun facts

  • St Patrick's Day is a public holiday on Montserrat, which is nicknamed the "Emerald Isle of the Caribbean".
  • Eruptions of the Soufrière Hills volcano from 1995 buried the capital, Plymouth, which has been abandoned ever since.

Flags often confused with Montserrat

  • British Virgin Islands: Montserrat and the British Virgin Islands are both Blue Ensigns whose badge shows a woman. Montserrat's woman wears green and holds a harp and a cross; the BVI's is dressed in white on a green shield surrounded by lamps.
  • Anguilla: Anguilla and Montserrat are both Caribbean Blue Ensigns with a badge in the fly. Anguilla's badge is three orange dolphins in a circle; Montserrat's is a woman in green with a harp and a cross.
