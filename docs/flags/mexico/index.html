Flag of Mexico

México

United Mexican States

What the flag looks like

Three equal vertical bands of green, white and red, with the coat of arms in the centre: a golden eagle perched on a prickly pear cactus devouring a snake.

What it means

Green is commonly said to represent hope, white unity and red the blood of national heroes; the eagle recalls the Aztec legend of the founding of Tenochtitlan.

How to remember it

Mexico is Italy plus an eagle eating a snake on a cactus, on a longer 4:7 rectangle; Ireland swaps the red for orange.

Fun facts

  • According to legend, the Aztecs founded Tenochtitlan, now Mexico City, where they saw an eagle on a cactus eating a snake.
  • Mexico celebrates Flag Day on 24 February.

Flags often confused with Mexico

  • Italy: Italy and Mexico are both green, white and red vertical stripes. Mexico has an eagle-and-cactus coat of arms in the centre and a longer shape; Italy's flag is plain.
  • Ireland: Ireland and Mexico both have vertical stripes starting green, then white. Ireland ends with orange and is plain; Mexico ends with red and has an eagle in the centre.
