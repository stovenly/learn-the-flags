Flag of Ishikawa

Officially Ishikawa Prefecture

Locally Ishikawa-ken · 石川県

What the flag looks like

A white emblem on a blue field: the kanji 石川 (Ishikawa) drawn in the shape of the Noto Peninsula.

What it means

The blue stands for the Sea of Japan and for the prefecture's greenery, clean water and clear air.

How to remember it

Ishikawa's white strokes on blue spell 石川 (Ishikawa) and trace the Noto Peninsula jutting into the sea.

Fun facts

  • Kanazawa, the capital, makes almost all of Japan's gold leaf.
  • Ishikawa is the only prefecture with no separate emblem: the design on its flag serves instead.

Flags often confused with Ishikawa
