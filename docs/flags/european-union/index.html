Flag of the European Union

Organizations

What the flag looks like

A blue field with a circle of twelve gold five-pointed stars in the centre.

What it means

The circle stands for unity, solidarity and harmony among the peoples of Europe; twelve is a symbol of completeness, not a count of members.

How to remember it

Twelve gold stars in a ring on blue, and always twelve however many countries join; Cape Verde's ring has ten stars and sits on stripes.

Fun facts

  • The Council of Europe adopted the flag in 1955, three decades before the European Communities took it up in 1985.
  • The number of stars has never followed the number of members: there were twelve when the EU had twelve members and there are still twelve with 27.

Flags often confused with the European Union

  • Cape Verde: The European Union and Cape Verde both have a ring of gold stars on blue. The EU has twelve stars on plain blue in the centre; Cape Verde has ten stars set towards the hoist over white and red stripes.
