Flag of Kochi

Officially Kochi Prefecture

Locally Kōchi-ken · 高知県

What the flag looks like

A white ring on a dark red field, with a red blade pointing up into its center: 土 (to), とさ (Tosa) and コ (ko) combined.

What it means

土 and とさ stand for Tosa, the old province, and コ for Kōchi; the blade points upward for progress, and the ring stands for peace and cooperation.

How to remember it

Kochi's white ring on dark red has a blade pointing up inside it; Kumamoto's white ring is open at the left.

Fun facts

  • Sakamoto Ryōma, a hero of the Meiji Restoration, was born in Kōchi.
  • The Shimanto River is often called the last clear stream in Japan.
