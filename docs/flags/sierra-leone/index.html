Flag of Sierra Leone

Officially Republic of Sierra Leone

What the flag looks like

Three horizontal stripes of green, white and blue.

What it means

Green represents agriculture and the mountains, white unity and justice, and blue the natural harbour of Freetown.

How to remember it

Sierra Leone reads top to bottom like the view from Freetown: green hills, white beaches, blue harbour; Gabon has yellow in the middle.

Fun facts

  • Freetown was founded in 1792 as a settlement for freed slaves.
  • The name comes from the Portuguese 'Serra Leoa', 'lion mountains'.

Flags often confused with Sierra Leone

  • Gabon: Gabon and Sierra Leone both have green on top and blue at the bottom. The middle stripe tells them apart: yellow for Gabon, white for Sierra Leone.
  • Lesotho: Lesotho and Sierra Leone both use blue, white and green horizontal stripes. Lesotho has blue on top and a black hat in a wider white stripe; Sierra Leone has green on top and no emblem.
  • Uzbekistan: Sierra Leone and Uzbekistan both use green, white and blue horizontal stripes. Sierra Leone has green on top and no emblem; Uzbekistan has blue on top, thin red lines and a crescent with stars.
