Flag of Kuwait

State of Kuwait

al-Kuwayt · الكويت

What the flag looks like

Three equal horizontal stripes of green, white and red, with a black trapezoid at the hoist whose shorter side faces the fly.

What it means

The colours come from a poem by Safi al-Din al-Hilli: white for good deeds, black for battles, green for fertile land and red for the blood on swords.

How to remember it

Kuwait's hoist is a black trapezoid, a triangle with its tip cut off; Jordan and Palestine have red triangles and the UAE a red bar.

Fun facts

  • The flag was first raised on 24 November 1961, a few months after independence from Britain.
  • The Kuwaiti dinar is the highest-valued currency unit in the world.

Flags often confused with Kuwait

  • United Arab Emirates: The UAE and Kuwait both have green, white and a fourth colour beside a hoist shape. The UAE has a red rectangle and black bottom stripe; Kuwait has a black trapezoid and red bottom stripe.
  • Jordan: Jordan and Kuwait both use black, white, green and red. Jordan has black on top with a red triangle holding a white star; Kuwait has green on top with a black trapezoid at the hoist.
  • Palestine: Kuwait and Palestine both have three stripes with a shape at the hoist. Kuwait is green on top with a black trapezoid; Palestine is black on top with a red triangle.
  • Sudan: Kuwait and Sudan both use green, white, red and black. Kuwait has green on top and a black trapezoid at the hoist; Sudan has red on top and a green triangle at the hoist.
