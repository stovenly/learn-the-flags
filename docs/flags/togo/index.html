Flag of Togo

Togolese Republic

What the flag looks like

Five horizontal stripes alternating green and yellow, with a red square canton in the upper hoist bearing a white five-pointed star.

What it means

Green stands for hope and agriculture, yellow for faith in work, red for the blood of the martyrs and the white star for hope; the five stripes represent the country's regions.

How to remember it

Togo is a Liberia-style 'lone star' flag in African colours: five green and yellow stripes with a white star in a red corner.

Fun facts

  • The flag was designed by Togolese artist Paul Ahyi.
  • Togo is a narrow strip of land with a coastline only about 56 km long.
