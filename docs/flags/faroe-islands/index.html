Flag of the Faroe Islands

Føroyar / Færøerne

Territories

What the flag looks like

A white field with a red Nordic cross edged in blue, its vertical arm set towards the hoist.

What it means

White stands for the foam of the sea and the bright Faroese sky, while red and blue are traditional Faroese colours.

How to remember it

The Faroes are the Nordic cross on a white background: Iceland and Norway put their cross on blue or red, but Merkið's field is white like sea foam.

Fun facts

  • The flag, called Merkið ("the banner"), was designed by Faroese students in Copenhagen and first raised in 1919.
  • British authorities recognised it on 25 April 1940 during the wartime occupation, and the date is now celebrated as Flag Day.
