Flag of Yamaguchi

Officially Yamaguchi Prefecture

Locally Yamaguchi-ken · 山口県

What the flag looks like

A white emblem on a brown field: the kanji 山口 (Yamaguchi) drawn as a bird flying toward the sun.

What it means

The circle and the bird flying toward the sun hide 山 (yama) and 口 (guchi), and stand for unity and progress.

How to remember it

Yamaguchi is the brown flag: its white bird is 山口 (Yamaguchi) flying with a mountain in its middle.

Fun facts

  • More prime ministers of Japan have come from Yamaguchi than from any other prefecture.
  • Shimonoseki, at its western tip, is famous for fugu, the pufferfish.
