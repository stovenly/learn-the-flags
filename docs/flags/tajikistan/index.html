Flag of Tajikistan

Tojikiston · Тоҷикистон

Republic of Tajikistan

What the flag looks like

Three horizontal stripes of red, white and green, with the white stripe twice as tall as the others, and a gold crown under an arc of seven gold stars in the centre.

What it means

Red represents unity and sovereignty, white purity and cotton, and green the bounty of nature; the crown and seven stars are linked to the people and to traditional ideas of happiness and perfection.

How to remember it

Tajikistan wears a gold crown with seven stars on its wide white band, red on top; Iran puts green on top, and Hungary has no emblem.

Fun facts

  • The crown is often linked to a folk etymology connecting the name "Tajik" with "taj", the Persian word for crown.
  • More than 90% of Tajikistan is mountainous, much of it in the Pamir range.

Flags often confused with Tajikistan

IranIran and Tajikistan both have green, white and red stripes. Iran has green on top and a red emblem; Tajikistan has red on top, a much wider white stripe and a gold crown under seven stars.HungaryHungary and Tajikistan are both red, white and green from the top. Hungary's stripes are equal and plain; Tajikistan's white stripe is wider and has a gold crown and stars.BulgariaBulgaria and Tajikistan are both horizontal white, green and red. Bulgaria has white on top and no emblem; Tajikistan has red on top and a gold crown and stars on a wide white middle.
