Flag of Guatemala

Officially Republic of Guatemala

What the flag looks like

Three equal vertical bands of sky blue, white and sky blue, with the coat of arms in the centre: a resplendent quetzal perched on a scroll dated 15 September 1821, over crossed rifles and swords inside a laurel wreath.

What it means

The blue bands represent the Pacific Ocean and the Caribbean Sea, and white represents peace and purity; the quetzal symbolises liberty.

How to remember it

Guatemala turns the Central American blue-white-blue sideways into vertical bands, with a green quetzal bird perched on crossed rifles.

Fun facts

  • The date on the scroll, 15 September 1821, is the day Central America declared independence from Spain.
  • Guatemala's currency, the quetzal, is named after the same bird that appears on its flag.

Flags often confused with Guatemala

  • El Salvador: Guatemala and El Salvador are both blue-white-blue with a central emblem. Guatemala's bands are vertical; El Salvador's are horizontal, with a triangle emblem.
  • Nicaragua: Guatemala and Nicaragua are both blue-white-blue with a central emblem. Guatemala's bands are vertical; Nicaragua's are horizontal, with a triangle emblem.
  • Honduras: Guatemala and Honduras are both blue-white-blue. Guatemala's bands are vertical with a coat of arms; Honduras' are horizontal with five blue stars.
  • Argentina: Argentina and Guatemala are both light blue, white and light blue with a central emblem. Argentina's stripes are horizontal with a gold sun; Guatemala's are vertical with a coat of arms.
